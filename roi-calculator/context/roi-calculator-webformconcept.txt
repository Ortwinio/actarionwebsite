Below are three webform concepts you can use on the ActARion site:

A Quick ROI snapshot (for homepage / landing pages)

A Full ROI wizard (for an in-depth calculator page)

A Lead-gen export form (same data, but focused on capturing details & sending a PDF)

I’ll describe each with:

Page/section purpose

Steps & field groups

Concrete field labels + help text

What the results section should show

You can hand this 1-to-1 to a designer/dev (or to your Cursor agent).

1. Quick ROI snapshot form (light version)

Use case
For homepage or a “SOP AR” landing page. Goal: in <1 minute a visitor sees “This pays back in ~X months”.
Minimal fields, lots of defaults.

Form title & intro

Title: “What could AR-guided SOPs save your plant?”

Subtitle: “Enter a few numbers and see a conservative estimate of savings from AR-guided procedures.”

Section A – Your operation

Industry

Type: Dropdown

Options: Food & Beverage, Pharma & Life Sciences, Packaging, Logistics, Other

Help: “Used only to pre-fill some assumptions. You can change them.”

Number of operators using SOPs

Name: N_op

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 25

Help: “Operators who perform the procedures you want to digitize in AR.”

New operators per year

Name: N_new

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 6

Help: “Rough estimate of new hires or internal transfers per year.”

Section B – Training & quality

Training hours per new operator today

Name: H_train_base

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 24

Help: “Formal training + shadowing before someone can run independently.”

Average error rate on these SOPs (%)

Name: Error_rate_base

Type: Slider or number input (0–20%)

Placeholder: 3

Help: “How many executions out of 100 lead to scrap, rework, or deviation?”

Average cost per error (€)

Name: Cost_per_error

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 250

Help: “Include scrap, rework time, extra checks, lost output.”

Section C – Downtime & cost of time

Downtime hours per year caused by SOP issues

Name: H_downtime_base_per_year

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 40

Help: “Unplanned stops due to incorrect setup, changeover, or procedure mistakes.”

Cost of one hour downtime (€)

Name: Cost_per_downtime_hour

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 2,000

Help: “Rough OEE cost or lost margin per hour line downtime.”

Average hourly cost of an operator (€)

Name: C_hour_op

Type: Number input

Placeholder: 40

Section D – AR adoption (simple)

AR adoption horizon

Type: Radio

Options: 1 year, 3 years (default), 5 years

Internally map to Horizon_years.

Expected AR impact (preset)

Type: Radio with short text:

Conservative (e.g. 30% fewer errors, 25% less training time, 25% less downtime)

Typical (50% fewer errors, 40% less training time, 40% less downtime)

Ambitious (70% fewer errors, 50% less training time, 50% less downtime)

Each preset sets:

Error_rate_AR

H_train_AR as % of base

Downtime_reduction_pct

Results panel (right side or bottom)

As soon as enough fields are filled, show:

Headline:
“Estimated annual savings: € X,XXX,XX”

Sub-points:

Training & onboarding: €…/year

Fewer errors & scrap: €…/year

Less downtime: €…/year

Secondary metrics:

3-year net benefit: €…

Estimated payback: ~ X months

CTA under the result:

Button: “Discuss your numbers with ActARion”
Small text under it: “We’ll validate the assumptions with you and build a project-sized ROI.”

2. Full ROI calculator – multi-step wizard

This goes on a dedicated page under Insights or Services: “AR SOP ROI calculator”.
Wizard style, 4–5 steps, so you can overlay more education.

Overall layout

Progress bar at top:

Your operation

Training & onboarding

Quality & downtime

Costs & adoption

Results

Right side: live-updated ROI summary with 2–3 key numbers.

Step 1 – Your operation

Title: “Tell us about your operation”

Fields:

Number of sites (N_sites)

Number of operators using SOPs (N_op)

New operators per year (N_new)

Average number of SOP executions per operator per year (Exec_per_op_per_year)

Help: “Across the procedures you want to digitize. For example: 400 setups/changeovers per operator per year.”

Optional: short toggle

“I don’t know – use an industry default.”

If checked, prefill based on industry choice.

Step 2 – Training & onboarding

Title: “Training and onboarding today”

Fields:

Training hours per new operator today (H_train_base)

Help: “Classroom, on-the-job and shadowing, until they can work independently.”

Training hours per new operator with AR (expected) (H_train_AR)

Default: 50–70% of above

Hint text: “In many AR projects, training time is reduced by 30–50%.”

Hourly cost of operators (C_hour_op)

Hourly cost of trainers/experts (C_hour_trainer)

Small info line (AR fact):

“Studies show operators can learn up to 4x faster using AR versus traditional manuals.”

Step 3 – Quality & downtime

Title: “Quality losses and downtime”

Fields:

Current error rate on these SOPs (%) (Error_rate_base)

Expected error rate with AR (%) (Error_rate_AR)

Option: slider; also allow “Use typical value: -50%”.

Average cost per error (€) (Cost_per_error)

Help: “Include scrap, rework, extra QC, complaints, lost batch, etc.”

Unplanned downtime hours per year caused by SOP issues (H_downtime_base_per_year)

Expected reduction in SOP-related downtime (%) (Downtime_reduction_pct)

E.g. slider: 0–80%

Cost per hour of downtime (€) (Cost_per_downtime_hour)

Optional info tooltip:

“AR-guided SOPs can reduce error-related downtime by 30–70%, depending on process complexity.”

Step 4 – Costs & adoption

Title: “Solution costs and adoption”

Block A – Licenses & hardware

Number of AR users (viewers + authors) (N_users)

License cost per user per year (€) (C_license_user_year)

Fixed license cost per site per year (€) (C_license_site_year) – optional

Number of devices (tablets / smart glasses) (N_devices)

Device cost per unit (€) (C_device_per_user)

Hardware lifetime (years) (Device_lifetime_years)

Block B – Implementation & content

One-time implementation cost (€) (Implementation_one_time)

Help: “Setup, integration and initial training.”

One-time AR content creation (€) (Content_initial)

Help: “Turning your most critical SOPs into AR workflows.”

Annual content maintenance (€) (Content_annual)

Internal project management hours per year (Internal_PM_hours)

Internal PM hourly rate (€) (Internal_PM_rate)

Block C – Adoption & financial assumptions

Adoption per year

Adoption_year1 (slider 0–1)

Adoption_year2 (slider 0–1)

Adoption_year3 (slider 0–1)

Business case horizon (Horizon_years)

Options: 3 years (default), 5 years

Discount rate (for NPV) (Discount_rate)

Default: 8–10%

Step 5 – Results

Title: “Your AR SOP business case”

Large result panel with:

Annual steady-state savings

“Estimated annual savings once fully adopted: € X,XXX,XXX”

Breakdown with bars or simple numbers:

Training & onboarding: €…

Fewer errors & scrap: €…

Less downtime: €…

Fewer on-site visits (if you include remote support later): €…

Investment and ROI

“Total 3-year investment: € …”

“Total 3-year benefits: € …”

“3-year ROI: Y%”

“Estimated payback: ~ X months”

Optional chart

Simple line or column chart with “Cumulative cashflow” per year (0–3).

CTA area

Text:
“Would you like us to validate this case with your real SOP data and build a rollout scenario?”

Button: “Schedule a 30-min ROI review with ActARion”

Optional checkbox: “Email me a PDF of this calculation.”

3. Lead-gen / export concept (same form, different framing)

Same fields as the full calculator, but presentation focused on:

Exportable PDF report

Sending results to ActARion as pre-qualification for a call

Additional/changed elements

At the end of the wizard (or in a side panel) add:

Name

Company

Work email

Country

Optional: “Preferred timeline for a pilot” (3–6 months, 6–12 months, just exploring)

Two actions:

Button 1: “Download ROI report (PDF)”

Button 2: “Share with ActARion and request a follow-up”

Confirmation message:

“We’ll review your numbers and propose 1–2 concrete pilot scenarios (e.g. one line, one site), including a detailed ROI with DeepSight AR workflows.”
