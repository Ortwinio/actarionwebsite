# Vision Pro vs HoloLens 2: which headset for industrial AR?

> Vision Pro vs HoloLens: Microsoft has discontinued HoloLens 2 and supports it with security fixes until 31 December 2027. Existing fleets can keep running, but new rollouts should plan on current devices such as Apple Vision Pro or Android XR headsets. Actarion supports all three, so your instructions move with you.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for vision pro vs hololens over a production hall](/images/compare-vision-pro-vs-hololens-1600.webp)

## What worries teams with a HoloLens fleet?

- No successor device is announced, so the roadmap is unclear.
- Spare devices and repairs get harder to source.
- Instructions built for one headset may not run on the next.
- Management asks whether the AR investment was a dead end.

## Conclusion first

HoloLens 2 is still a capable see-through headset for existing deployments until its support ends. For new projects, Apple Vision Pro offers a current, high-resolution passthrough experience, and Android XR headsets are an emerging option. Choose based on the task and your site, and keep your content device-independent.

## How do the criteria compare?

**Availability.** HoloLens 2 is end of life. Buying more units now is a short-term decision.

**Display type.** Optical see-through gives a direct view of the room, which some safety teams prefer. Video passthrough shows the room through cameras; image quality on Vision Pro is high, but you should assess each task with HSE.

**Battery and comfort.** Vision Pro uses an external battery; Apple states up to 2.5 hours of general use, longer when plugged in. Plan for task-length sessions.

**Content.** The bigger risk is locked-in content. If instructions are tied to one device, a device change means re-authoring.

## When do you choose what?

1. **Keep HoloLens 2** if you have a working fleet; use the time until end of 2027 to plan migration.
2. **Choose Apple Vision Pro** for new projects needing high visual detail, training and remote expert support.
3. **Evaluate Android XR** if you prefer the Android ecosystem or need a different price or form factor.
4. **Use tablets** where headsets are not needed or not allowed.
