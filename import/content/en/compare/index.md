# Work instruction comparisons: choose the right approach

> This work instruction comparison hub helps operations, quality and L&D leaders choose between paper and digital instructions, training systems and frontline tools, tablets and AR glasses. Each comparison starts with a conclusion, then a criteria table. Actarion turns your SOPs into digital, AR-guided work instructions and helps you pick the right mix for a pilot.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a control room; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for work instruction comparison](/images/compare-1600.webp)

## Why is choosing the right setup so hard?

- Every vendor says its device or platform fits every task.
- Paper still works, so the business case for change feels vague.
- Training, quality and operations each own a different system.
- A wrong hardware choice ends up in a drawer after the pilot.

## How do we write these comparisons?

1. We start with the conclusion, so you can stop reading after one paragraph.
2. We compare on criteria that matter on the shop floor: speed, errors, safety, audit, cost of change.
3. We explain each criterion in plain language.
4. We end with "when to choose what", including when *not* to choose Actarion.

We do not publish claims about other vendors that we cannot verify. Hardware facts are checked against public sources and dated. If a fact changes, we update the page and the date.

## How does Actarion help?

- **AI authoring:** your existing SOPs become step-by-step digital work instructions.
- **Device choice:** the same instruction runs on a tablet, smartphone or AR glasses.
- **Audit trail:** every execution logs time, confirmations and deviations.
- **Pilot with fixed KPIs:** you test the comparison on your own line before you scale.

## FAQ

### Are these comparisons neutral?

We aim for fairness. Where Actarion is not the best fit, we say so. Vendor-specific claims are only made when they are publicly verifiable.

### Do I have to choose one device?

No. Most sites mix tablets for routine tasks with AR glasses for complex or hands-free tasks.

### Can I test before I decide?

Yes. A pilot on one use case with fixed KPIs is the fastest way to decide.

### How current are these comparisons?
We review them when products or support status change. The date at the top of each page shows the last update.
