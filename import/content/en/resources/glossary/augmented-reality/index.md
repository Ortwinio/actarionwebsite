# What Is Augmented Reality (AR) in Industry?

> Augmented reality (AR) overlays digital information, such as arrows, labels or 3D models, on the real world, seen through a tablet camera or AR glasses. In industry, AR shows operators exactly where and how to act. Actarion uses AR to guide work instructions step by step on the actual machine.

![Pen drawing for the glossary term augmented reality: tablet overlaying a digital gear on a machine](/img/illustraties/resources-glossary-augmented-reality.svg)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Operators lose time matching a drawing to the real machine.
- Rare tasks are forgotten between occurrences.

## What is augmented reality (AR) in industry?

Augmented reality is technology that adds computer-generated content to a live view of the physical environment, anchored to real objects.

AR can run on tablets and smartphones (hand-held) or on headsets and smart glasses (hands-free). Typical industrial uses are assembly, maintenance, training, inspection and remote support.

## Key elements

1. Recognize the object or location
2. Anchor digital content to it
3. Guide the user through the next step

A technician points a tablet at a pump. An arrow highlights the valve to close first, and the step card shows the torque value.

## FAQ

### Do I need glasses for AR?

No. Tablets and smartphones can show AR through the camera view.

### What is the difference between AR and VR?

AR adds to the real world; VR replaces it with a virtual one.

### Is AR mature enough for the shop floor?

Yes for many tasks; test devices per use case in a pilot.
