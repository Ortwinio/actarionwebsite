# Glossary: shop-floor, quality and AR terms explained

> This glossary explains the terms operations, quality and HSE teams use every day, from SOP and SMED to LOTO, GMP and augmented reality. Each entry gives a short definition, a shop-floor example and related terms. Actarion uses these concepts to turn SOPs into AR-guided digital work instructions.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a production hall; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for manufacturing glossary](/images/resources-glossary-1600.webp)

## Why a shared vocabulary?

- Operations, quality and IT use the same word for different things.
- New hires drown in abbreviations on day one.

## Which terms are covered?

| Term | In one line |
|---|---|
| [SOP](/en/resources/glossary/sop/) | An SOP is a controlled document that describes the standard way to carry out a process. |
| [work instruction](/en/resources/glossary/work-instruction/) | A work instruction is a document that tells an operator exactly how to perform one task, step by step. |
| [connected worker](/en/resources/glossary/connected-worker/) | A connected worker is a frontline employee who is digitally linked to instructions, systems and colleagues while doing the job. |
| [SMED](/en/resources/glossary/smed/) | SMED is a lean method, developed by Shigeo Shingo, to reduce changeover time. |
| [OEE](/en/resources/glossary/oee/) | OEE is a KPI that shows which share of planned production time results in good output at full speed. |
| [poka-yoke](/en/resources/glossary/poka-yoke/) | Poka-yoke is a Japanese term for 'mistake-proofing'. |
| [skills matrix](/en/resources/glossary/skills-matrix/) | A skills matrix (also called a competency or qualification matrix) is an overview of people versus tasks, showing each person's level per task. |
| [LOTO](/en/resources/glossary/loto/) | LOTO is a procedure to isolate energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, mechanical, thermal) and secure them with locks and tags before work starts. |
| [line clearance](/en/resources/glossary/line-clearance/) | Line clearance is a verification step between batches that confirms all previous product, components, labels and records have been removed from the line. |
| [GMP](/en/resources/glossary/gmp/) | GMP is a quality system for manufacturing, laid down in regulations such as EU GMP (EudraLex Volume 4) and US 21 CFR Parts 210/211. |
| [CAPA](/en/resources/glossary/capa/) | CAPA is a structured process for corrective actions (fix an existing problem) and preventive actions (avoid a potential problem). |
| [first time right](/en/resources/glossary/first-time-right/) | First time right is a quality KPI: the share of output that meets requirements at the first attempt. |
| [augmented reality](/en/resources/glossary/augmented-reality/) | Augmented reality is technology that adds computer-generated content to a live view of the physical environment, anchored to real objects. |
| [mixed reality](/en/resources/glossary/mixed-reality/) | Mixed reality is a form of extended reality in which virtual objects are spatially mapped to the real environment and respond to it. |
| [smart glasses](/en/resources/glossary/smart-glasses/) | Smart glasses are head-worn displays with a camera and sensors that present information in or near the user's field of view. |
| [remote assistance](/en/resources/glossary/remote-assistance/) | Remote assistance (also remote expert support) is a video-based connection between a frontline worker and an expert, enriched with drawings or AR markers in the worker's view. |
| [knowledge capture](/en/resources/glossary/knowledge-capture/) | Knowledge capture is the systematic recording of skills, experience and tacit knowledge from experts in a reusable form. |
| [digital twin](/en/resources/glossary/digital-twin/) | A digital twin is a digital model of a physical object or system that is linked to it through data, so the model reflects its current state. |
| [MES](/en/resources/glossary/mes/) | An MES is an information system that tracks and documents the conversion of raw materials into finished goods, in real time. |
| [Werkerassistenzsystem](/en/resources/glossary/werkerassistenzsystem/) | A Werkerassistenzsystem (worker assistance system) is a digital system that supports employees during manual work with context-specific information and checks. |
