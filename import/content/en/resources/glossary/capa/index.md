# What Is CAPA? Corrective & Preventive Action

> CAPA, or corrective and preventive action, is the quality process that investigates the root cause of a deviation, corrects it and prevents recurrence. It is required in GMP and ISO-based systems. Actarion speeds up CAPA by pushing updated work instructions to every station and showing step-level deviation data.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a conveyor line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for what is capa](/images/resources-glossary-capa-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- The same deviation returns because the fix never reached the floor.
- Root-cause analysis lacks data about how the task was done.

## What is CAPA?

CAPA is a structured process for corrective actions (fix an existing problem) and preventive actions (avoid a potential problem).

A CAPA usually runs from detection via root-cause analysis to action and effectiveness check. A common action is changing an SOP or work instruction, and retraining staff.

## Key elements

1. Describe the deviation and investigate the root cause
2. Define and implement corrective and preventive actions
3. Verify effectiveness

## Example from the shop floor

Labels are applied crooked on one line. Root cause: unclear alignment step. The work instruction gets a photo and a check, and the deviation stops.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between corrective and preventive action?

Corrective fixes a problem that happened; preventive stops one that could happen.

### Who owns CAPA?

Quality usually owns the process; operations owns many of the actions.

### How do you check CAPA effectiveness?

Measure whether the deviation recurs over a defined period.
