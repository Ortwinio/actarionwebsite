# What Is GMP? Good Manufacturing Practice

> GMP, or good manufacturing practice, is the set of regulations and guidelines that ensures medicines, food and similar products are produced consistently and controlled to quality standards. Documented procedures and records are central. Actarion supports GMP with controlled digital work instructions and an audit trail per execution.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for what is gmp over a conveyor line](/images/resources-glossary-gmp-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Batch records contain gaps and late entries.
- Training evidence is scattered across systems.

## What is GMP?

GMP is a quality system for manufacturing, laid down in regulations such as EU GMP (EudraLex Volume 4) and US 21 CFR Parts 210/211.

GMP requires qualified staff, validated processes, controlled documents and complete records. 'If it is not documented, it did not happen' is a well-known GMP principle.

## Key elements

1. Approved procedures and instructions
2. Trained, qualified personnel
3. Complete, traceable records

## Example from the shop floor

In a tablet plant, each batch follows approved instructions. Every step, deviation and line clearance is recorded and reviewed before release.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between GMP and cGMP?

cGMP means 'current' GMP, the term used by the US FDA.

### Does GMP apply to food?

Yes, GMP principles also apply in food and feed, alongside HACCP.

### Are digital records allowed under GMP?

Yes, if the system meets data integrity requirements such as EU GMP Annex 11.
