# What Is Remote Assistance? Definition & Use

> Remote assistance is live support in which an off-site expert sees the technician's view through a tablet, smartphone or smart glasses and guides them with voice, annotations or AR markers. It cuts travel and downtime. Actarion offers remote assistance within the same platform as the work instructions.

![Pen drawing for the glossary term remote assistance: expert tablet connected to a machine with a highlighted spot](/img/illustraties/resources-glossary-remote-assistance.svg)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- A machine stands still while waiting for the service engineer.
- Experts spend days travelling for short fixes.

## What is remote assistance?

Remote assistance (also remote expert support) is a video-based connection between a frontline worker and an expert, enriched with drawings or AR markers in the worker's view.

The expert can freeze the image, draw on it and share documents. Sessions can be recorded and turned into new instructions, which also supports knowledge capture.

## Key elements

1. Worker starts a call from the task
2. Expert sees the live view and annotates
3. Solution is logged and reused

## Example from the shop floor

A night-shift operator faces an unknown alarm. A specialist joins via video, marks the sensor to check, and the line restarts within the hour.
