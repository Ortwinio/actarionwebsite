# What Is a Skills Matrix? Definition & Example

> A skills matrix is a table that shows which employees are qualified for which tasks or machines, and at what level. It supports shift planning, training and audits. Actarion keeps the skills matrix current in the DeepSight console, based on real executions of digital work instructions.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a production hall; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for what is a skills matrix](/images/resources-glossary-skills-matrix-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- The matrix is an outdated spreadsheet on one person's laptop.
- Planners discover gaps only when someone is ill.

## What is a skills matrix?

A skills matrix (also called a competency or qualification matrix) is an overview of people versus tasks, showing each person's level per task.

Typical levels range from 'in training' to 'can train others'. The matrix shows single points of failure, training needs and who may sign off a task. Auditors often ask for it.

## Key elements

1. List tasks or machines as columns
2. List employees as rows
3. Rate each cell with a clear level and date

## Example from the shop floor

A team leader sees that only two operators can change over line 3. She schedules a third for guided changeovers this month.

- Digital, step-by-step work instructions generated from your SOPs with AI.

## FAQ

### What levels does a skills matrix use?

Often four: learning, can do with help, independent, can train others.

### How often should it be updated?

Continuously, or at least after every training or qualification.

### Is a skills matrix required for ISO 9001?

ISO 9001 requires evidence of competence; a skills matrix is a common way to show it.
