# What Is a Werkerassistenzsystem? Meaning

> Werkerassistenzsystem is the German term for a worker assistance system: technology that guides operators step by step at the workstation, using screens, projection, pick-to-light or AR glasses, and records the work. It is close to a connected worker platform. Actarion delivers worker assistance with AI-generated, AR-guided instructions.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for werkerassistenzsystem over an electrical cabinet](/images/resources-glossary-werkerassistenzsystem-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- High variant counts overwhelm assembly workers.
- German-speaking sites search for this term, not 'connected worker'.

## What is a Werkerassistenzsystem?

A Werkerassistenzsystem (worker assistance system) is a digital system that supports employees during manual work with context-specific information and checks.

Forms include screen-based guidance, projection onto the workbench, pick-to-light systems and AR glasses. In DACH industry, the term is common in assembly and Industry 4.0 discussions.

## Key elements

1. Detect the order or variant
2. Show the right step at the workstation
3. Confirm and record the result

## Example from the shop floor

At an assembly station, a screen shows the next step for the current variant and the right bin lights up; the step is confirmed before the next one starts.

## FAQ

### How do you translate Werkerassistenzsystem?

Worker assistance system; in English, 'connected worker platform' is close.

### Is it only for assembly?

No. It is also used in maintenance, inspection and logistics.

### Does it require AR?

No. Many systems use screens or projection; AR is one option.
