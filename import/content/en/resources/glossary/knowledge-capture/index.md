# What Is Knowledge Capture? Definition

> Knowledge capture is the process of recording the practical know-how of experienced employees, often tacit knowledge, so it stays in the organization and can be reused. It is urgent where staff retire. Actarion captures expert tasks on video and turns them with AI into digital work instructions.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a production hall; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for what is knowledge capture](/images/resources-glossary-knowledge-capture-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Your best technician retires in two years.
- Tricks and checks live only in people's heads.

## What is knowledge capture?

Knowledge capture is the systematic recording of skills, experience and tacit knowledge from experts in a reusable form.

Tacit knowledge is hard to write down because experts do it automatically. Recording the expert while working, then structuring the result, works better than interviews alone.

## Key elements

1. Record the expert performing the task
2. Structure into steps with AI support
3. Review, approve and publish

## Example from the shop floor

A senior fitter records a rare overhaul with a headset camera. The video is split into steps, checked by the fitter and published as an instruction.

## FAQ

### What is tacit knowledge?

Know-how people use without being able to explain it easily.

### Which tasks to capture first?

Rare, critical tasks known by few people.

### Is knowledge capture the same as documentation?

It is a source for documentation, focused on expert practice.
