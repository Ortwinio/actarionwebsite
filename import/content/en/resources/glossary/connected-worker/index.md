# What Is a Connected Worker? Definition

> A connected worker is a frontline employee who uses digital tools, such as tablets, AR glasses and apps, to get instructions, data and expert help during the task, and whose work is recorded. Actarion delivers connected worker solutions: AI-generated work instructions, AR guidance, skills tracking and remote assistance.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a warehouse; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for what is a connected worker](/images/resources-glossary-connected-worker-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Operators work with outdated paper and no feedback loop.
- Expert knowledge is a phone call away, if someone picks up.

## What is a connected worker?

A connected worker is a frontline employee who is digitally linked to instructions, systems and colleagues while doing the job. A connected worker platform is the software that makes this possible.

The idea is simple: bring the right information to the worker at the right moment, and bring data from the task back to the organization. Typical building blocks are digital work instructions, checklists, skills management and remote support.

## Key elements

1. Guidance: digital instructions and checklists
2. Data: execution, deviations, readings
3. Support: remote experts and skills management

## Example from the shop floor

A maintenance technician opens the task on a tablet, follows the steps, logs readings and calls a remote expert when a part looks worn.

## FAQ

### Is a connected worker the same as a wearable user?

Not necessarily. Many connected workers use a tablet or smartphone; glasses are one option.

### Who benefits from connected worker tools?

Operators, technicians, team leaders, quality and HR/L&D.

### Does it replace an MES?

No. It complements the MES with guidance and task-level data.
