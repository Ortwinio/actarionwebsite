# What Is First Time Right? Definition & KPI

> First time right (FTR) is the percentage of products, tasks or jobs completed correctly the first time, without rework, repair or repeat visits. It reflects process quality and instruction clarity. Actarion raises FTR with step-by-step digital instructions, built-in checks and AR guidance at the point of work.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a robotic assembly line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for first time right](/images/resources-glossary-first-time-right-1600.webp)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Rework eats capacity that was planned for production.
- Field technicians need a second visit to finish a job.

## What is first time right?

First time right is a quality KPI: the share of output that meets requirements at the first attempt. It is also called first pass yield in production.

FTR is calculated as good units at first attempt divided by total units. Low FTR often points to unclear instructions, missing skills or variant complexity.

## Key elements

1. Count units or jobs started
2. Count those correct without rework
3. Divide and track per shift, product or step

## Example from the shop floor

Of 500 assembled units, 470 pass the final test without rework. FTR is 94%.

## FAQ

### Is first time right the same as first pass yield?

They are closely related and often used interchangeably.

### What is a good FTR?

It depends on the process; track the trend against your own baseline.

### How do work instructions improve FTR?

Clear steps and checks prevent errors before they become rework.
