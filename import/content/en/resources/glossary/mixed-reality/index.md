# What Is Mixed Reality? Definition vs AR and VR

> Mixed reality (MR) blends digital content with the physical world so holograms stay anchored and can interact with real surfaces and hands. Headsets such as Apple Vision Pro and HoloLens 2 deliver MR. Actarion uses mixed reality headsets for hands-free work instructions, training and remote assistance.

![Pen drawing for the glossary term mixed reality: headset and a floating hologram cube above a workbench](/img/illustraties/resources-glossary-mixed-reality.svg)

## Why does it matter on the shop floor?

- Teams are confused by AR, MR and XR labels.
- Hardware choice is driven by hype, not the task.

## What is mixed reality?

Mixed reality is a form of extended reality in which virtual objects are spatially mapped to the real environment and respond to it.

In practice, the line between AR and MR is blurry. MR usually refers to headsets that map the room and track hands and eyes. 'Spatial computing' is Apple's term for a similar idea.

## Key elements

1. Map the room
2. Place holograms on real surfaces
3. Interact with hands, eyes or voice

## Example from the shop floor

A trainee wearing a headset sees a 3D model of a valve placed on the workbench and practises the disassembly sequence by hand.

## FAQ

### Is mixed reality the same as AR?

Closely related. MR usually means richer spatial interaction on a headset.

### Which devices support mixed reality?

Examples are Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2 and Android XR headsets.

### Is HoloLens 2 still available?

Microsoft has discontinued it; security support runs until 31 December 2027.
