## Why do shop-floor documents fail?

- Every team uses its own version, so nobody knows which checklist is current.
- Paper forms get skipped, filled in afterwards or lost before the audit.
- New operators get a binder instead of guidance at the machine.
- Experienced people carry the real procedure in their heads.

## How do you use these templates?

1. **Pick one process.** Start with the task that causes the most downtime, rework or audit findings.
2. **Fill it in at the machine.** Walk through the task with the most experienced operator.
3. **Test it with a new operator.** If they get stuck, the template is not finished.
4. **Put it under document control.** Give it an owner, a version number and a review date.
5. **Measure.** Track changeover time, first-time-right or onboarding time before and after.

## FAQ

### Are these manufacturing templates really free?
Yes. You can use and adapt every template on these pages. Downloads in Word, Excel and PDF are free as well.

### Can I use the templates for ISO 9001 or GMP audits?
They follow common practice for ISO 9001, ISO 45001 and GMP documentation. Always add your own document control and have your quality or HSE team approve the final version.

### Which template should I start with?
Start where errors or downtime hurt most. For many plants that is the changeover checklist, the LOTO procedure or the work instruction template.
