# Lockout Tagout Procedure Template: Machine-Specific and OSHA-Aligned

> A lockout tagout procedure is a machine-specific document that tells authorized employees how to shut down, isolate, lock, release stored energy and verify zero energy before service or maintenance. This free lockout tagout procedure template follows the structure of OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 and common EU practice (EN ISO 14118). Actarion guides each LOTO step with AR and logs it.

![Pen drawing of an electrical cabinet with a padlock and tag next to a tablet showing the lockout tagout procedure steps](/img/illustraties/resources-templates-lockout-tagout-procedure.svg)

## Why do lockout procedures fail?

- A generic plant-wide procedure does not show where the isolation points are on this machine.
- Stored energy (pneumatics, springs, gravity) is forgotten.
- The verification step, the try-start, is skipped under time pressure.
- There is no record of who locked out what, and when.

## What is a LOTO procedure and when is it required?

Lockout/tagout (LOTO) is the practice of isolating all energy sources and securing them with personal locks so a machine cannot start or move during servicing. In the US, OSHA 1910.147 requires a documented energy control procedure for each machine with more than one energy source, plus training and annual periodic inspections. In the EU, employers must prevent unexpected start-up under the Work Equipment Directive 2009/104/EC and national rules; EN ISO 14118 describes the technical principles. This template is a starting point. Your HSE team must validate it per machine.

## How do you fill in the template?

1. **One procedure per machine.** Walk the machine with maintenance and HSE. Photograph every isolation point.
2. **Energy table:** include all types: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity, springs, thermal, chemical. Missing one is the most common cause of injuries during LOTO.
3. **Locks:** every person working on the machine applies their own lock. Never lock out for someone else.
4. **Verification:** always try to start the machine after isolation. Return the control to off afterwards.
5. **Shift change:** describe how locks are transferred so protection is never interrupted.
6. **Review:** inspect the procedure in use at least annually, as OSHA requires.

## FAQ

### What are the steps of lockout tagout?
Prepare, notify, shut down, isolate, apply locks and tags, release stored energy and verify zero energy. Release follows its own sequence.

### Is a written LOTO procedure required for every machine?
Under OSHA 1910.147, yes, unless the machine meets all conditions of the exception, such as a single energy source that can be locked by one device.

### What is the difference between lockout and tagout?
Lockout physically prevents operation with a lock. Tagout is a warning tag only and is allowed under OSHA only when a device cannot be locked out, with additional safeguards.

### How often must LOTO procedures be inspected?
OSHA requires a periodic inspection at least annually by an authorized employee other than those using the procedure.
