# Maintenance Schedule Template: Plan Every PM Task for the Year

> A maintenance schedule template is a planning sheet that lists every asset, its preventive maintenance tasks, their frequency and when each task is due over the year. This free maintenance schedule template contains an asset register, a task plan and a 52-week overview with status tracking. Actarion links each planned task to an AR-guided checklist that logs completion.

![Pen drawing of a maintenance schedule calendar with marked tasks, a gear and a tablet showing planned maintenance orders](/img/illustraties/resources-templates-maintenance-schedule.svg)

## Why does the maintenance plan slip?

- Tasks live in separate spreadsheets per technician.
- Production gets no warning, so planned stops are cancelled.
- Overdue tasks are invisible until something breaks.
- Nobody can show the auditor that the plan was actually executed.

## What is a maintenance schedule and when do you use it?

A maintenance schedule is the overview of **when** each preventive task happens. The [preventive maintenance checklist](/en/resources/templates/preventive-maintenance-checklist/) describes **what** is done during each task. Use the schedule to plan workload, align stops with production and track compliance (planned vs. completed).

## How do you fill in the maintenance schedule?

1. **Asset register:** start with critical (A) assets: those that stop production or create safety risk when they fail.
2. **Frequency:** take the manufacturer's interval as a baseline. Use running hours or cycles where usage varies.
3. **Machine stop:** mark tasks that need a stop. Plan these together with production, ideally at changeovers or weekends.
4. **Spread the load:** avoid putting all monthly tasks in week 1. Level the workload per trade.
5. **Status:** mark each task as done, late or missed. PM compliance = tasks done on time ÷ tasks planned.
6. **Review:** each quarter, check failures against the plan and adjust intervals.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a maintenance schedule and a maintenance checklist?
The schedule shows when tasks happen. The checklist describes what to do during each task.

### How do you calculate PM compliance?
Divide the number of PM tasks completed on time by the number planned in the period, times 100.

### Should I use Excel or a CMMS?
Excel works for a small number of assets. Once you have many assets or multiple technicians, a CMMS gives better planning and history.

### How often should the maintenance schedule be reviewed?
Quarterly for status, and at least annually for intervals based on failure data.
