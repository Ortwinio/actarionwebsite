# Skills Matrix Template: See Who Can Do What on Every Shift

> A skills matrix is a grid that shows, per employee, which tasks they can perform and at what level. This free skills matrix template uses a 0–4 scale, a target per task and a gap row, so team leaders can plan shifts and training. Actarion keeps the skills matrix current automatically from completed AR-guided work instructions.

## Why do shift plans fail on skills?

- One sick operator and nobody else can run the changeover.
- Training records sit in HR, not with the team leader.
- The matrix on the wall is months out of date.
- Auditors ask for proof of qualification and it takes a day to find.

## What is a skills matrix and when do you use it?

A skills matrix (also called training matrix or competency matrix) maps people against tasks and shows the qualification level for each. Use it to plan shifts, spot single points of failure and prioritize training. ISO 9001 clause 7.2 requires you to determine competence and keep evidence; a skills matrix is the most common way to do that.

## How do you fill in the skills matrix?

1. **Tasks:** list tasks, not job titles. Use the same names as your work instructions, e.g. "Changeover line 3" or "Daily PM filler".
2. **Levels:** only a trainer (level 4) or team leader assesses. Level 2 requires a practical sign-off, not just attendance.
3. **Target:** set per task how many qualified people you need per shift. A common rule is at least three per critical task.
4. **Gap:** target minus actual. Any task with one qualified person is a single point of failure.
5. **Training plan:** turn the largest gaps into named training actions with dates.
6. **Update:** review monthly and after every training or staff change.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a skills matrix and a training matrix?
Mostly naming. A training matrix often tracks completed courses; a skills matrix tracks demonstrated ability per task.

### What scale should a skills matrix use?
A 0–4 scale works well in production. Keep the definitions short and tied to observable behavior.

### How often should the skills matrix be updated?
At least monthly, and immediately after training, sign-offs or staffing changes.

### Does ISO 9001 require a skills matrix?
ISO 9001 requires evidence of competence. It does not prescribe a format, but a skills matrix is the most common evidence.
