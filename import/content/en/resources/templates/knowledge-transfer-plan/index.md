# Knowledge Transfer Plan: Capture Expert Know-How Before It Leaves

> A knowledge transfer plan is a structured plan to capture the critical know-how of an experienced employee and pass it to successors before they retire, move or leave. This free knowledge transfer plan template maps tasks, ranks knowledge risk, plans capture and transfer, and verifies successors. Actarion captures expert tasks as AR-guided instructions.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a production hall; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for knowledge transfer plan](/images/resources-templates-knowledge-transfer-plan-1600.webp)

## Why is knowledge lost when an expert leaves?

- The real procedure lives in one person's head, not in the SOP.
- Handover starts two weeks before the farewell party.
- The expert explains, but nobody records it in a usable form.
- Rare tasks (annual overhaul, specific faults) are not covered at all.

## What is a knowledge transfer plan and when do you use it?

A knowledge transfer plan identifies which knowledge is critical, who holds it, how it will be captured and who must be able to apply it by when. Start at least 12 months before a known departure, and for any task where only one person is qualified. Your [skills matrix](/en/resources/templates/skills-matrix-template/) shows those single points of failure.

## How do you fill in the plan?

1. **Inventory:** interview the expert and their team leader. Ask: "What do people call you for?" and "What would go wrong if you were away for a month?"
2. **Risk:** prioritize undocumented tasks with high impact and only one holder. Start there.
3. **Capture at the machine:** record the expert doing the task, step by step, with the "why" behind each key point. Office interviews miss details.
4. **Turn it into instructions:** a video alone is not enough. Convert it into a [work instruction](/en/resources/templates/work-instruction-template/) or troubleshooting guide.
5. **Reverse shadowing:** the successor performs, the expert watches and corrects. This is where transfer actually happens.
6. **Verify:** knowledge is transferred only when the successor can do the task alone.

## FAQ

### When should knowledge transfer start?
Ideally 12 months before a known departure, and immediately for any task with only one qualified person.

### What is the best way to capture tacit knowledge?
Record the expert doing the task at the workplace and ask why at each step. Then turn it into a structured instruction.

### How do you know the transfer worked?
When the successor performs the task independently, observed and signed off, ideally more than once.

### Who owns the knowledge transfer plan?
The line manager of the expert, supported by HR or L&D. The expert and successor do the work.
