## Why does 5S fade after the kick-off?

- The workshop was a big clean-up, but no standard followed.
- Audits are done by different people with different criteria.
- Findings are noted but nobody owns the actions.
- Scores are not visible on the floor, so nobody feels ownership.

## What is a 5S audit and when do you do it?

5S is a lean method for workplace organization. A 5S audit is a short, scored inspection of one area against fixed criteria. Do it monthly per area, rotate auditors, and post the score on the team board. The audit is the "Sustain" engine: without it, areas drift back.

## How do you fill in the 5S audit?

1. **Walk, don't sit.** Do the audit in the area, with a team member present.
2. **Score honestly.** Use the definitions; a 4 means fully in place with no exceptions.
3. **Photograph findings.** Photos make actions concrete and show progress next time.
4. **Limit actions.** Pick three to five actions with an owner and due date, not twenty.
5. **Rotate auditors.** Cross-audits between areas prevent blind spots. Calibrate together once a quarter.
6. **Show the trend.** Post total and per-S scores on the team board.

## FAQ

### What are the 5S's?
Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardize and Sustain (in Japanese: seiri, seiton, seiso, seiketsu, shitsuke).

### How often should a 5S audit be done?
Monthly per area is common, with short daily or weekly self-checks by the team.

### Who should perform 5S audits?
A rotating auditor from another area, together with a member of the audited team.

### What is a good 5S score?
There is no universal benchmark. Focus on the trend per area and close the actions.
