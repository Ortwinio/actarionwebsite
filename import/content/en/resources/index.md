# Resources for digital work instructions

> The Actarion resource hub brings together free templates, practical guides, an ROI calculator, a glossary, whitepapers, events and videos about digital work instructions. It helps operations, maintenance, quality and HR teams turn paper SOPs into clear step-by-step instructions, with or without AR, and build a business case for a pilot.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for work instruction resources over process equipment](/images/resources-1600.webp)

## What do you need before you digitize instructions?

- A clear format for SOPs and work instructions.
- Arguments and numbers for your management team.
- Shared terms between operations, quality and IT.

## How Actarion helps

- Every template can be converted into a digital AR instruction.
- The ROI calculator feeds directly into a pilot proposal.
- Guides follow the Actarion approach: SOP → AI → AR → audit-proof.

## FAQ

### Are the resources free?
Yes. Templates, guides and the glossary are free to use.

### Can I use the templates without Actarion?
Yes. They work on paper or in Word; digitizing them is the next step.
