# Events and webinars on AR work instructions

> At Actarion events you see AI-generated, AR-guided work instructions in action. We host webinars, join industry trade fairs and run on-site sessions where your team tries a step-by-step instruction on a tablet or AR glasses. Upcoming dates are listed below; you can also request a session at your own site.

![A technician holds an iPad up to an electrical cabinet; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar work instructions webinar](/images/resources-events-1600.webp)

## Why see it live?

- AR is hard to judge from a video.
- Your team wants to try it with their own hands.
- You want to ask questions about your own process.

## How do you request an on-site session?

1. Send a request via the contact form, topic Sales & Demo.
2. We agree on a use case from your own plant.
3. Your team tries the instruction on tablet or AR glasses.

## How Actarion helps

- Live demos on tablet and AR glasses, not just slides.
- Examples from your own sector: food, pharma, manufacturing.
- A follow-up intake to map your first use case.
