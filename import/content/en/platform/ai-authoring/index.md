# AI SOP generator: from paper procedure to digital instruction

> An AI SOP generator is software that turns source material, such as a Word SOP, a PDF or a recorded walkthrough, into a structured, step-by-step procedure. Actarion's AI authoring converts your existing SOPs into digital work instructions with images, checks and warnings. Your experts review and approve before anything reaches the shop floor.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a conveyor line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ai sop generator](/images/platform-ai-authoring-1600.webp)

## Why does writing work instructions take so long?

- Your engineers write procedures in the evening, after their real work. The backlog keeps growing.
- Every author uses a different format, so operators get different levels of detail.
- Existing SOPs are long text documents. Operators need short steps with a picture.
- Each change means rewriting, reformatting and re-approving the whole document.

## What does AI authoring do?

AI authoring reads your source material and proposes a structured instruction. It splits long text into single actions, suggests checks and highlights safety warnings. On the DeepSight platform, AI also proposes a standard sequence when several experts do the same job differently.

The result is a draft, not a final instruction. A trainer refines it in a no-code studio, and a responsible owner approves it. That keeps the expert in control of what operators see.

## How does the AI SOP generator work?

1. **Collect sources.** Upload a Word or PDF SOP, photos of the workstation, or a recording made during real work.
2. **Generate the draft.** AI splits the content into steps, adds a title per step and suggests checks, tools and warnings.
3. **Refine in the studio.** A trainer adds images, video or 3D content and adjusts the wording. No coding needed.
4. **Review and approve.** The process owner approves the version. The approval is recorded.
5. **Publish.** The instruction goes to tablets and AR glasses. Updates sync to every device.

## Paper SOP versus AI-generated instruction

| Aspect | Paper or PDF SOP | AI-generated digital instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Long text, varies by author | One action per step, consistent template |
| Visuals | Few, often outdated | Image, video or AR overlay per step |
| Update | Rewrite and redistribute | Edit once, sync to all devices |
| Proof of execution | Signature at the end | Confirmation and timestamp per step |
| Authoring time | Hours to days per SOP | Faster first draft [CIJFER TE BEVESTIGEN] |
