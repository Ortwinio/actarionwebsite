# Security and GDPR for digital work instructions

> Digital work instructions process company know-how and data about employees, so security and GDPR compliance are part of every rollout. This page explains how Actarion handles data protection, hosting, access control and the EU AI Act. Certifications and hosting details are confirmed per contract and listed here once verified.

![A phone held up to a control room shows AR steps and a confirmed check for gdpr work instructions](/images/platform-security-1600.webp)

## What do IT and the works council want to know?

- Where is our data stored, and who can access it?
- Does the system track individual operators, and how is that data used?
- Are camera images from the shop floor stored?
- Does AI in the platform fall under the EU AI Act?

## What data does the platform process?

| Data | Example | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Content | SOPs, images, videos, 3D models | Creating and showing work instructions |
| User data | Name, role, language, login | Access and personalization |
| Execution data | Time per step, confirmations, deviations | Audit trail and analytics |
| Skills data | Levels, sign-offs | Skills matrix |
| Media from capture or remote support | Video and photos | Knowledge capture and help at the machine |

## How is the data protected?

1. **Agreements.** A data processing agreement under the GDPR is part of the contract. [TE BEVESTIGEN: DPA template]
2. **Hosting.** [TE BEVESTIGEN: hosting provider and EU data location]
3. **Access.** Role-based permissions decide who sees content and execution data. [TE BEVESTIGEN: SSO and MFA]
4. **Certification.** [TE BEVESTIGEN: ISO 27001 or SOC 2 status of Actarion and DeepSight]
5. **Transparency.** DeepSight publishes a Trust Center and privacy policy. [TE BEVESTIGEN: link]

## The EU AI Act and AI authoring

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) sets rules based on the risk of an AI system. Using AI to draft work instructions, with human review before publication, is in our assessment not one of the high-risk uses listed in the Act. AI systems used to evaluate or monitor workers can be high-risk, so how execution data is used for individual assessment needs attention. [TE BEVESTIGEN: legal assessment of AI Act classification]
