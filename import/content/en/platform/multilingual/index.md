# Multilingual work instructions: one procedure, every language

> Multilingual work instructions are digital procedures that each operator can open in their own language, while the content and approval stay the same. Actarion delivers multilingual work instructions on tablet and AR glasses. Combined with visuals and AR overlays, they reduce misunderstandings in mixed teams and with temporary staff.

![Pen drawing: one work instruction on a tablet fans out into three step cards in different languages](/img/illustraties/platform-multilingual.svg)

## Where does language get in the way?

- Your team speaks five languages, and the SOP exists in one.
- Temporary staff nod at the briefing but did not understand the safety step.
- A colleague translates on the fly, and details get lost.
- Every translated PDF is a separate document that drifts out of date.

## What does the multilingual module do?

One instruction is the master. Translations are linked to it, so a change in the master is visible in every language. The operator selects a language on the device and sees the same steps, checks and warnings.

Visual content does a lot of the work. An arrow on the right valve needs no translation. Text then only has to carry what the image cannot show.

## How do multilingual work instructions work?

1. **Write the master.** Create or generate the instruction in your working language.
2. **Translate.** Generate translations for the languages your teams need. [TE BEVESTIGEN: machine translation and supported languages]
3. **Review.** A bilingual colleague checks safety-critical wording.
4. **Publish.** Operators choose their language on tablet or AR glasses.
5. **Update once.** When the master changes, linked translations are flagged for update.

## Separate translated PDFs versus multilingual instructions

| Aspect | Translated PDFs | Actarion multilingual |
|---|---|---|
| Versions | One file per language | One master with linked translations |
| Updates | Each file by hand | Change flagged in every language |
| Visuals | Often missing | Same images and AR overlays for all |
| Audit trail | Per document, if any | Same execution log, whatever the language |

## What does it bring each role?
