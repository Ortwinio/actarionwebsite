# Remote assistance with AR: the expert sees what you see

> Remote assistance with AR is a live video connection in which a remote expert sees the operator's view and draws annotations that appear on the equipment. Actarion remote support works from a tablet, smartphone or AR glasses and starts from within a work instruction. Breakdowns get solved without waiting for a specialist to travel.

![Pen drawing: an operator's AR glasses at a pump connect to a laptop where a remote expert marks the part in cyan](/img/illustraties/platform-remote-support.svg)

## What does a breakdown cost you today?

- The line stands still while you wait for the one technician who knows this machine.
- Phone calls go back and forth: "Which cable? The left one?"
- A supplier specialist has to travel for a problem that took ten minutes to fix.
- Night and weekend shifts have no expert on site.

## What is AR remote support?

Remote support connects the operator at the machine with an expert elsewhere: a colleague at another site, a central engineering team or a machine supplier. The expert sees a live camera image and can mark parts, draw arrows and share documents. On AR glasses, the operator keeps both hands free.

## How does remote assistance with AR work?

1. **Start the call.** The operator taps "help" inside the work instruction, or calls an expert from the contact list.
2. **Share the view.** The camera of the tablet or AR glasses streams to the expert.
3. **Annotate live.** The expert circles the right part or draws an arrow; the mark stays on the object.
4. **Fix and confirm.** The operator carries out the fix and confirms the step.
5. **Learn from it.** The solution can be added to the work instruction, so the next person does not need a call.

## Phone call versus AR remote support

| Aspect | Phone or video call | AR remote support |
|---|---|---|
| Shared view | Description in words | Live view of the operator |
| Pointing | "The one on the left" | Annotation on the equipment |
| Hands | One hand holds the phone | Hands-free on AR glasses |
| Record | None | Linked to the task and audit trail [TE BEVESTIGEN] |

## What does it bring each role?

| Role | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Operator | Help at the machine, without leaving the task |
| Team leader | Shorter stoppages; less dependence on one expert |
| Quality / HSE | Fewer improvised fixes; the solution is documented |
| HR / L&D | New staff can take on harder tasks with a safety net |
