# Skills matrix software that updates from real work

> Skills matrix software shows which employee is trained, qualified or certified for which task. In Actarion, the skills matrix is part of the console and is fed by real work: completed AR work instructions, assessments and sign-offs. Team leaders see gaps at a glance, and quality has proof of qualification for every audit.

![Pen drawing: a screen with a skills matrix where cyan cells show certified employees](/img/illustraties/platform-skills-management.svg)

## Why is your skills matrix always out of date?

- It lives in a spreadsheet that one person updates when they have time.
- You only find out nobody is qualified for a machine when that one operator is sick.
- Training records and real performance are in different systems.
- Auditors ask for proof of qualification, and you search for paper certificates.

## What is a skills matrix?

A skills matrix, also called a competency or training matrix, is a table with employees in rows and tasks or skills in columns. Each cell shows a level, for example: not trained, in training, independent, can train others. It is the basis for shift planning, training plans and audits such as ISO 9001.

## What does Actarion skills management do?

The console collects usage and performance data from every executed instruction. It links that data to the skills matrix, so levels are based on what people actually did. You can correlate training with productivity and safety.

## How does skills matrix software work in Actarion?

1. **Define the skills.** Link each skill to one or more digital work instructions.
2. **Set the levels.** Agree on criteria per level, such as number of supervised runs or a sign-off.
3. **Train on the job.** Operators follow the instruction with AR guidance; each run is logged.
4. **Sign off.** A team leader or trainer confirms the level in the console.
5. **Plan and close gaps.** Filter the matrix by line, shift or skill, and assign training where coverage is thin.
