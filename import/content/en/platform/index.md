# Work instruction platform: from SOP to AR guidance and audit trail

> A work instruction platform is software that creates, distributes and records step-by-step procedures for frontline work. The Actarion work instruction platform uses AI to turn your existing SOPs into digital instructions, guides operators with AR on a tablet or AR glasses, and logs every execution as audit evidence. The technology comes from partner DeepSight.

![A phone held up to a control room shows AR steps and a confirmed check for work instruction platform](/images/platform-1600.webp)

## Why do paper procedures fail on the shop floor?

- Instructions sit in binders and PDFs. Operators work from memory, and errors and scrap follow.
- Every new hire needs weeks of shadowing from your most experienced people.
- Updating a procedure means reprinting, redistributing and hoping the old version is gone.
- At audit time you chase signatures instead of showing proof.

## How does the work instruction platform work?

1. **Capture.** Upload an SOP or record an expert doing the job on a tablet or AR glasses.
2. **Author.** AI structures the content into steps with images and checks. A trainer refines it in a no-code studio and approves it.
3. **Execute.** Operators follow the steps with AR guidance on the device that suits the task.
4. **Measure.** The console collects execution data, skills and deviations in one place.
5. **Improve.** You update the instruction once, and every device gets the new version.
