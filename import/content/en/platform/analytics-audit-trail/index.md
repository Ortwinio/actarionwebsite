# Work instruction audit trail: proof of every step, automatically

> A work instruction audit trail is a record of every execution: who did which step, when, on which version, and with what result. Actarion builds the audit trail automatically while operators follow digital work instructions. The same data shows where time is lost and where errors start.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for work instruction audit trail over a control room](/images/platform-analytics-audit-trail-1600.webp)

## Why are audits so stressful?

- Proof of execution is a signature at the bottom of a paper form, if it was signed at all.
- You cannot show which version of the procedure the operator used.
- Deviations are noted on paper and never analyzed.
- You know a task takes too long but not which step causes it.

## What does the audit trail record?

Every time an operator runs a digital work instruction, the platform logs:

- **Who** carried out the task and their qualification level.
- **What** instruction and which version was used.
- **When** each step started and was confirmed.
- **Evidence**, such as photos, measured values or checks.
- **Deviations**, including skipped or repeated steps and remarks.

## How does the work instruction audit trail work?

1. **Execute.** The operator confirms each step on tablet or AR glasses.
2. **Log.** Time, user, version and evidence are stored per step.
3. **Review.** Quality filters executions by line, product, shift or operator.
4. **Analyze.** The console shows time per step, deviations and trends.
5. **Improve.** You change the instruction; the new version is tracked from that moment.

## Paper record versus digital audit trail

| Aspect | Paper record | Actarion audit trail |
|---|---|---|
| Granularity | One signature per task | Confirmation per step |
| Version | Unclear | Logged with every execution |
| Evidence | Attached later, if at all | Photos and values in the step |
| Search | Archive boxes | Filter and export in seconds |
| Process insight | None | Time per step and deviation trends |

## What does it bring each role?

| Role | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Operator | No separate paperwork after the job |
| Team leader | Sees where the team gets stuck |
| Quality / HSE | Audit evidence ready, for ISO 9001, GMP or customer audits |
