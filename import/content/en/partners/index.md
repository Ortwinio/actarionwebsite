# Actarion partners: technology and implementation

> Actarion combines one technology platform with local implementation partners. DeepSight supplies the AR and AI platform for digital work instructions. Hardware from Apple, Microsoft and Google brings the instructions to the work. Potentify (HR and skills) and Simply Change (change management) help the new way of working stick on the shop floor.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for ar work instructions partners over an electrical cabinet](/images/partners-1600.webp)

## Why do AR projects stall after the demo?

- The technology works, but nobody owns the content.
- HR and L&D are not involved, so training records stay separate.
- Supervisors are not on board, so adoption drops after the pilot.

## Who are Actarion's partners?

| Partner | Type | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [DeepSight](/en/partners/deepsight/) | Technology | AR-first connected worker platform: authoring, library, player, data |
| Apple | Hardware | Apple Vision Pro for spatial computing |
| Microsoft | Hardware | HoloLens 2 for hands-free mixed reality |
| Google | Hardware | Android XR technologies for AR in the field |
| [Potentify](/en/partners/potentify/) | Implementation | HR, learning design and skills matrices |
| [Simply Change](/en/partners/simply-change/) | Implementation | Change management and roll-out, FMCG experience |

## How do the partners work together?

1. **Actarion** leads the project: use case discovery, pilot and scale-up.
2. **DeepSight** provides the platform for creating and running AR instructions.
3. **Hardware partners** supply the devices that fit the task.
4. **Implementation partners** connect the project to HR, training and daily routines.

## How Actarion helps

- One point of contact for platform, devices and implementation.
- Local teams in Benelux, DACH and the Nordics.
- A pilot with fixed KPIs before any scale-up.
- Your own people learn to build and maintain instructions.

## FAQ

### Is Actarion a reseller of DeepSight?
Actarion is DeepSight's European partner. DeepSight provides the platform; Actarion handles local implementation, content guidance and change management.

### Do I need all partners in my project?
No. We bring in a partner only when your use case needs it, for example Potentify when skills records must link to HR.

### Can my company become a partner?
Yes, if you serve industrial customers in Europe. See [become a partner](/en/partners/become-a-partner/).
