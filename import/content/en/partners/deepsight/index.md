# DeepSight in Europe, implemented by Actarion

> DeepSight is an AR-first connected worker platform, based in Montreal, for creating and running AI- and AR-guided work instructions, training and remote support. Actarion is DeepSight's European partner. Actarion implements the platform in Benelux, DACH and the Nordics: from use case selection and content to pilot, roll-out and change management.

![Pen drawing: monitor with cyan step cards in a studio, rolled out to a tablet on a stand and AR glasses](/img/illustraties/partners-deepsight.svg)

## What do European teams need besides a good platform?

- Support in their own language and time zone.
- Help turning existing SOPs into usable AR content.
- A clear pilot that proves value before the budget for scale.

## What does the DeepSight platform do?

- **No-code authoring:** build step-by-step AR procedures without programming.
- **Central library:** keep work instructions and safety content in one place.
- **Cross-platform player:** run instructions on phones, tablets, Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2 and other supported devices.
- **Data capture:** task completion times, quiz results, photos, videos and operator feedback.
- **Remote support:** connect workers with experts across sites and shifts.

## How does an implementation run?

1. **Future-of-work briefing** for your management team.
2. **Use case discovery:** pick two or three tasks with clear impact.
3. **Pilot with fixed KPIs** on one line or site.
4. **Scale and enablement:** more sites, your own authors.

## What do you need to prepare?

A DeepSight implementation through Actarion goes faster when a few things are ready before the kick-off:

- **Two or three candidate tasks** with a clear problem: errors, long onboarding or frequent expert calls.
- **Source material** for those tasks: current SOPs, photos, drawings or short videos.
- **A process owner** who reviews and approves the digital instructions.
- **Devices and network:** which tablets or AR glasses you use, and Wi-Fi coverage at the workstations.
- **IT and security contacts** for user management, hosting and data questions `[TE BEVESTIGEN: hosting, datalocatie en SSO-ondersteuning DeepSight]`.
- **A baseline** for the KPIs you want to improve, so the pilot result is measurable.

Actarion helps you fill gaps in this list during the use case discovery.

## How Actarion helps
