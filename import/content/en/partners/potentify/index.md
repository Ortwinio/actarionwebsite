# Potentify: HR partner for AR learning and skills

> Potentify is Actarion's HR implementation partner for learning and skills. Together they connect AI- and AR-guided work instructions to HR, learning and talent strategy: AR-enabled onboarding that aims to shorten time to competence, AR training data linked to learning records, and skills programs that use AR tasks as evidence.

![Pen drawing: skills matrix with one cyan cell and a rising staircase as a learning path](/img/illustraties/partners-potentify.svg)

## Why does shop-floor training stay disconnected from HR?

- Training happens at the machine, but records live in a separate system.
- Skills matrices are spreadsheets nobody updates.
- Proving competence for an audit takes days.

## What does Potentify bring?

- Expertise in HR, learning design and performance management.
- Experience designing competency frameworks and skills matrices.
- Support aligning digital work instructions with onboarding, upskilling and compliance.

## Typical joint projects

| Project | Goal |
|---|---|
| HR-led AR onboarding for new technicians | Shorter time to competence |
| Reskilling teams for new equipment, product lines or safety standards | Faster changeover of skills |
| Skills and certification programs with AR tasks as evidence | Audit-proof qualification |

## How Actarion helps

- Every completed AR instruction counts toward the skills matrix.
- Training data flows to your learning records.
- Continuous learning paths combine on-the-job AR guidance with formal training.

No. Potentify joins when the project must link to HR processes and skills records.
