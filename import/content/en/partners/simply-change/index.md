# Simply Change: change partner for AR roll-outs

> Simply Change is Actarion's change management and implementation partner, with experience in FMCG and operational transformation. Together they help AI and AR work instructions move beyond pilots and become embedded in daily work at scale, through stakeholder engagement, coaching of supervisors, structured roll-outs and measurement of adoption and impact.

![Pen drawing: three factories in which a cyan screen is rolled out step by step, each with a check mark](/img/illustraties/partners-simply-change.svg)

## Why do pilots fail to become the standard?

- The pilot team is enthusiastic, the next plant is not.
- Roles and SOPs are not updated, so old habits return.
- Nobody measures adoption, so support fades.

## What does Simply Change do?

- **Change management:** stakeholder engagement, communication and coaching of supervisors and team leads.
- **Structured roll-outs:** AR work instructions across multiple plants or warehouses.
- **Process alignment:** SOPs, roles and responsibilities aligned so the new way of working lasts.
- **Measurement:** adoption and impact, such as error reduction and training time.

## Typical joint projects

1. AR-guided changeovers and setups on FMCG production lines.
2. AR work instructions in warehouses and logistics.
3. Continuous improvement programs that use AR execution data.

## How Actarion helps

- A roll-out plan per site, based on the pilot KPIs.
- Supervisors trained to coach with AR instructions.
- Adoption dashboards from the execution data.

## FAQ

### When does Simply Change join a project?
Usually when the pilot succeeds and you plan to scale to more lines or sites.

### Does Simply Change only work in FMCG?
Their background is FMCG and operational transformation; the approach also fits other production and logistics settings.
