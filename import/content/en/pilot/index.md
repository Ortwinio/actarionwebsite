# AR work instructions pilot in four phases

> An Actarion pilot proves the value of AI-generated, AR-guided work instructions on one line or site before you scale. It runs in four phases: a future-of-work briefing, use case discovery, a pilot with fixed KPIs and scale-up with enablement of your own team. You start with a free intake call.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a robotic assembly line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar work instructions pilot](/images/pilot-1600.webp)

## Why do many digitization pilots fail?

- The use case is chosen for the demo effect, not the business impact.
- Nobody measured the baseline, so the result is an opinion.
- After the pilot, nobody in-house can maintain the content.

## What are the four phases of the pilot?

| Phase | What happens | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Future-of-work briefing | Session with management on skills shortage, AR and AI on the shop floor | Shared picture and ambition |
| 2. Use case discovery | Workshop on the floor; pick two or three tasks with high impact | Use case shortlist with baseline KPIs |
| 3. Pilot with fixed KPIs | Build instructions from your SOPs, run them on one line or site | Measured effect against the baseline |
| 4. Scale and enablement | More lines and sites; train your own authors | Standard way of working, owned by your team |

## Which KPIs do you measure?

- Time to competence for new hires
- Changeover time and first-time-right rate
- Errors, rejects and deviations per 1,000 executions
- Unplanned downtime linked to procedures
- Time spent on audit preparation

## What do you need to provide?

- A sponsor in operations and a key user on the floor.
- Existing SOPs, photos or videos of the chosen tasks.
- Access to the line for capture and testing.
- Baseline data for the chosen KPIs.

## How Actarion helps

- AI authoring turns your SOPs into the first instructions quickly.
- AR guidance on tablets or AR glasses, whichever fits the task.
- The audit trail delivers the after-measurement automatically.
- Your own authors are trained so you are not dependent on us.
