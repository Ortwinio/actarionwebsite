# Book a demo of AR work instructions

> In an Actarion demo you see how an existing SOP becomes an AI-generated, step-by-step work instruction, how an operator follows it with AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses, and how every execution lands in the audit trail and skills matrix. We tailor the demo to a use case from your own sector.

![Pen drawing: laptop with a video call and a cyan step card, next to AR glasses and a check mark](/img/illustraties/demo.svg)

## What do you want to know after a demo?

- Will my operators actually use this?
- How much work is it to build instructions?
- Does it fit our quality system and IT?

## What does the demo cover?

1. Your situation and use case (10 min).
2. From SOP to digital instruction with AI authoring.
3. Execution with AR guidance on tablet or glasses.
4. Audit trail, dashboards and skills matrix.
5. Questions and next steps.

## Which fields are on the demo form?

- First and last name (required)
- Work email (required)
- Company (required)
- Role
- Sector: manufacturing, food, pharma, energy, logistics, other
- Use case you want to see: onboarding, changeover, maintenance, remote support, inspections
- Preferred device: tablet, AR glasses, no preference
- Preferred date and time zone
- Consent to process data under the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/) (required)

## How Actarion helps

- A demo on your own use case, not a generic slide deck.
- Clear next step: use case discovery or pilot.
- Local contact in Benelux, DACH or the Nordics.

## FAQ

### Is the demo free?
Yes. The demo and the intake that follows are free.

### Can I bring my own SOP?
Yes. Send it in advance and we use it as the example.
