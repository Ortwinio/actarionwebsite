# Operator onboarding: new hires productive faster with AR guidance

> Operator onboarding is the period in which a new hire learns to run tasks safely and alone. With Actarion, new operators learn on the job with AI-generated, step-by-step instructions and AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses. Every completed step feeds the skills matrix, so you know when someone is ready.

![Pen drawing of a workbench from above with a tablet showing a learning path, AR glasses and a workpiece for operator onboarding](/img/illustraties/use-cases-onboarding-training.svg)

## Why does onboarding take so long?

- Training depends on a buddy who has little time and explains it his own way.
- Binders and PDFs are outdated, so new hires learn the unofficial version.
- High turnover and temp staff mean you start over every few months.
- You cannot see who is actually qualified for which task.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Time to autonomy | Days until the operator works without a buddy |
| Coaching hours per new hire | Load on your experienced staff |
| Errors in the first 90 days | Quality of learning |
| Skills matrix coverage | Share of tasks with at least two qualified people |

## How does AR-guided onboarding work?

1. **Capture the expert once.** An experienced operator performs the task while photos, video and notes are captured.
2. **AI builds the instruction.** Actarion converts the SOP and recordings into clear steps with visuals, in the operator's language.
3. **Learn by doing.** The new hire performs the real task with AR guidance: the next step appears on the machine.
4. **Confirm and check.** Critical steps require a confirmation or photo. The system logs time and deviations.
5. **Sign off in the skills matrix.** After enough correct runs, the team lead approves the skill.

## Hardware you need

- **Tablet** on a stand or in hand: lowest threshold, good for most tasks.
- **AR glasses** (Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2, Android XR) when the task needs both hands.
- Wi-Fi on the shop floor. Offline use is possible `[TE BEVESTIGEN: offline-modus]`.

## FAQ

### Does this replace classroom training?
No. Safety basics and theory still have a place. AR guidance replaces the part where a buddy shows the task over and over.

### How do we know someone is qualified?
The system records each run. The team lead approves the skill based on correct runs and observations, and the skills matrix updates.

### Does it work for temp workers?
Yes. Instructions are available in several languages and guide each step, which suits short assignments.

### Who keeps the instructions current?
Your process owner. Edits are made in the studio and published to all devices at once, with version history.
