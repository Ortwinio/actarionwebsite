# AR picking: the right item, first time, even for new staff

> AR picking means pickers see the location, item and quantity in their field of view through AR glasses, with both hands free. Actarion applies the same approach to other logistics tasks such as kitting, packing and loading: step-by-step instructions with AR guidance and a record of every order.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a warehouse; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar picking](/images/use-cases-logistics-1600.webp)

## What causes errors in the warehouse?

- Seasonal and temp staff who do not know the layout or the items.
- Similar items in adjacent locations.
- Paper pick lists or handheld screens that pull attention away from the shelf.
- Special packing or kitting rules that only a few people know.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Pick accuracy | Share of order lines without errors |
| Picks per hour | Productivity |
| Onboarding time for temp staff | Time until normal pace |
| Returns due to wrong items | Customer impact |

## How does AR picking work?

1. **Receive the order** from the WMS `[TE BEVESTIGEN: WMS-koppelingen]`.
2. **Guide to the location.** The glasses show the next location and the route.
3. **Highlight the item.** The right bin or box is marked in AR.
4. **Confirm by scan.** The picker scans the item or location to confirm.
5. **Pack by instruction.** Kitting or packing rules appear step by step.
6. **Log the order.** Time, picker and deviations are stored.

## Results you can expect

Fewer picking errors, faster onboarding of temp staff and better data per order. Customer figures: `[CIJFER TE BEVESTIGEN]`.

## Hardware you need

- **AR glasses** for hands-free picking.
- **Tablet on a cart** as an alternative for kitting or packing stations.
- Barcode scanning via device camera or ring scanner `[TE BEVESTIGEN: ondersteunde scanners]`.

## How to start a pilot

Choose one zone or one complex process, such as kitting for the production line. Measure pick accuracy and onboarding time for temp staff before and during the pilot. [Plan your pilot](/en/pilot/).

## Common mistakes in a pilot

- **Starting with the whole warehouse.** One zone or one complex process gives clearer data and faster learning.
- **No baseline for pick accuracy.** Without it you cannot prove the effect on errors.
- **Ignoring the WMS link.** Agree early how orders reach the device, even if the pilot uses a manual export.
- **Only testing with experienced pickers.** The biggest gain is usually with temp and seasonal staff, so include them.

## FAQ

### Is AR picking only for large warehouses?
No. It often pays off first in complex processes like kitting for production, where errors stop the line.

### Do pickers get tired of wearing glasses?
Shift length and comfort differ by device. We test this during the pilot with your team.

### Does it work with our WMS?
Integration is project-specific `[TE BEVESTIGEN: WMS-koppelingen]`.
