# AR maintenance: preventive maintenance done right, every time

> AR maintenance means technicians see maintenance steps projected on the asset through a tablet or AR glasses. Actarion converts your preventive maintenance procedures into step-by-step AR instructions. Technicians follow the right sequence, record readings on the spot and leave a complete digital record for every job.

![A technician holds an iPad up to process equipment; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar maintenance](/images/use-cases-preventive-maintenance-1600.webp)

## Why does preventive maintenance still miss things?

- The task list in the CMMS says "inspect pump", not how.
- Paper checklists come back incomplete, late or illegible.
- Senior technicians retire and take the asset knowledge with them.
- Failures recur because steps were skipped under time pressure.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| PM schedule compliance | Share of planned jobs done on time |
| Mean time between failures (MTBF) | Effect of better maintenance on reliability |
| Wrench time per job | Time spent working versus searching |
| Repeat failures | Faults that return within 30 days |
| Completeness of records | Share of jobs with all readings logged |

## How does AR maintenance work?

1. **Start from the work order.** The planned job opens the right instruction `[TE BEVESTIGEN: CMMS-koppelingen]`.
2. **Identify the asset.** The technician scans a code or the asset is recognized.
3. **Follow the steps on the asset.** AR shows where to isolate, what to check and which tool to use.
4. **Record readings.** Values, photos and remarks are captured in the step itself.
5. **Flag deviations.** A value out of range triggers a follow-up action.
6. **Close with proof.** The job record shows who did what and when.

## Results you can expect

Higher schedule compliance, fewer repeat failures and records you can show any auditor. Customer figures: `[CIJFER TE BEVESTIGEN]`. In anonymized mining and transport cases, maintenance was a core use case alongside traceability ([see cases](/en/customers/)).

## Hardware you need

- **AR glasses** when both hands are on the asset.
- **Rugged tablet** for rounds and readings.
- Optional: ATEX-rated devices for hazardous zones `[TE BEVESTIGEN: ATEX-hardware]`.

## FAQ

### Does this replace our CMMS?
No. The CMMS plans the work. Actarion guides the execution and returns the result `[TE BEVESTIGEN: koppeling met CMMS]`.

### Can we use the OEM manual?
Yes. AI extracts the relevant steps from the manual. Your own experience is added as tips per step.

### What if there is no network at the asset?
Instructions can be prepared for offline use, depending on the device `[TE BEVESTIGEN: offline-modus]`.
