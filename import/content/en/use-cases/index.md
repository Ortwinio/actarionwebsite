# Use cases for AR work instructions on the shop floor

> AR work instructions pay off wherever people repeat critical tasks: onboarding, assembly, changeovers, maintenance, inspections, lockout/tagout, shutdowns and picking. Actarion turns your existing SOPs into step-by-step digital instructions with AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses. Every run is logged, so you get audit evidence without extra paperwork.

![A technician holds an iPad up to an electrical cabinet; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar work instructions use cases](/images/use-cases-1600.webp)

## Where does it hurt today?

- New operators need weeks before they work alone, and your experienced people spend that time coaching.
- Changeovers and maintenance jobs take longer every time someone skips or guesses a step.
- Auditors ask for proof that the procedure was followed, and the proof is a paper form with a signature.
- Knowledge sits in the heads of colleagues who retire in the next five years.

## How do you choose the first use case?

1. **List the tasks with the highest cost of error.** Think stoppages, scrap, safety incidents or audit findings.
2. **Check frequency.** A task done daily by several people gives faster learning than a yearly job.
3. **Check the SOP.** If a usable procedure exists, AI can convert it in days instead of weeks.
4. **Pick one or two KPIs** you already measure, so the baseline is clear.
5. **Start a pilot** on one line or one team, then scale.

## FAQ

### Which use case should we start with?
Start where errors are expensive and the task is frequent, often changeovers, onboarding or preventive maintenance. A short use-case discovery session helps you rank the options.

### Do we need AR glasses for every use case?
No. Most use cases start on a tablet. Glasses help when people need both hands free, such as in maintenance or picking.

### Can we combine several use cases?
Yes. Once SOPs are digital, the same platform covers training, execution and audits. Most customers add use cases after the first pilot.

### How long does a pilot take?
Usually a few weeks to a few months, depending on scope `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`.
