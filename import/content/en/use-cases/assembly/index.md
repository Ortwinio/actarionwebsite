# Assembly work instructions that prevent errors at every variant

> Assembly work instructions describe, step by step, how to build a product or module correctly. Actarion turns your existing assembly instructions into digital steps with AR guidance: the operator sees which part goes where, in which order and with which torque. Every build is logged, so quality can trace each unit.

![Pen drawing of an assembly where AR highlights the next bolt to fit, following digital assembly work instructions](/img/illustraties/use-cases-assembly.svg)

## What goes wrong in assembly today?

- Many variants and small batches: operators build from memory and mix up versions.
- Engineering changes reach the line late, so the old drawing is still in use.
- Rework and field returns trace back to a missed step or a wrong part.
- Experienced assemblers carry the tricks that are not in any document.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| First-time-right rate | Share of units built without rework |
| Assembly errors per 1,000 units | Direct quality cost |
| Cycle time per variant | Effect of clear instructions on speed |
| Time to implement an engineering change | How fast the line follows the design |

## How do digital assembly instructions work?

1. **Import the existing instruction** (Word, PDF, drawings, photos).
2. **AI splits it into steps** with one action per step, parts list and tools.
3. **Add AR markers:** the next part, bolt or connector is highlighted on the product.
4. **Add checks:** torque values, photo confirmation or a scan of the part number at critical steps.
5. **Execute and log:** each unit gets a record with time, operator and deviations.
6. **Update once:** a change in the studio reaches every station at once.

## Results you can expect

Fewer variant errors, faster learning on new products and traceability per unit. Figures from customers: first-time-right improvement `[CIJFER TE BEVESTIGEN]`. We set the baseline together during the pilot.

## Hardware you need

- **Tablet on a stand** at the workstation: hands free, screen at eye level.
- **AR glasses** for large products or work where the operator moves around.
- Optional: barcode scanner or connection to MES `[TE BEVESTIGEN: beschikbare integraties]`.

## How to start a pilot

Choose one product family with frequent errors or many variants. Bring the current instructions and your rework data. We build the digital version, run it on one station and compare against the baseline. [Plan your pilot](/en/pilot/).

## FAQ

### Can we use our CAD data?
In many cases yes, for visuals and AR overlays `[TE BEVESTIGEN: ondersteunde CAD-formaten]`. Photos and short videos also work well.

### What about very short cycle times?
For cycles under a minute, a tablet with large visuals works better than glasses. Steps can be grouped so the operator is not slowed down.

### How do we handle engineering changes?
You change the instruction once in the studio. Every station shows the new version and the old one is archived.
