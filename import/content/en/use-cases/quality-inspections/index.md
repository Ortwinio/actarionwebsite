# Digital inspection checklist: catch defects at the source

> A digital inspection checklist replaces the paper form with guided checks on a tablet or AR glasses. Actarion builds the checklist from your inspection plan, shows each inspection point on the product and records values, photos and pass/fail per step. Quality gets complete, searchable data instead of ticked boxes.

![Pen drawing of a flange, caliper and tablet with a digital inspection checklist](/img/illustraties/use-cases-quality-inspections.svg)

## What is wrong with paper inspections?

- Boxes are ticked without the check really being done.
- Values are written down but never analyzed.
- Inspectors interpret criteria differently, so results vary by shift.
- During an audit, finding the right form takes hours.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Defects found at the source | Errors stopped before the customer |
| Inspection completeness | Share of checks with a valid result |
| Time per inspection | Efficiency of the inspector |
| Customer complaints | End result of better inspections |

## How does a digital inspection work?

1. **Convert the inspection plan.** AI builds steps from your control plan or paper checklist.
2. **Define the criteria.** Each step has a limit value, a reference photo or a pass/fail rule.
3. **Guide the inspector.** AR shows where to measure and what good looks like.
4. **Capture evidence.** Values, photos and remarks are stored with time and inspector.
5. **Act on deviations.** A failed check triggers a hold or a follow-up action.
6. **Analyze trends.** The dashboard shows which checks fail most.

More reliable inspections, less variation between inspectors and audit evidence at the push of a button. Customer figures: `[CIJFER TE BEVESTIGEN]`. Anonymized mining, food and pharma cases all included inspections or evaluations ([see cases](/en/customers/)).

## Hardware you need

- **Tablet** for most inspections.
- **AR glasses** for large objects or inspections that need both hands.
- Optional: connected measuring tools `[TE BEVESTIGEN: koppeling meetmiddelen]`.

## How to start a pilot

Take one inspection with many findings or audit remarks. Download our [quality inspection checklist](/en/resources/templates/quality-inspection-checklist/), fill it in for your product and we digitize it. [Plan your pilot](/en/pilot/).

## Common mistakes in a pilot

- **Copying the paper form one-to-one.** Use the switch to remove checks nobody uses and to add tolerances and photos where they matter.
- **No clear rule for deviations.** Define what happens when a value is out of tolerance before go-live.
- **Forgetting the gauges.** Decide whether values are typed in or come from connected measuring tools `[TE BEVESTIGEN: koppeling meetmiddelen]`.
