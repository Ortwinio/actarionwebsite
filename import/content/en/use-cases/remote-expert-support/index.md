# Remote expert support: the specialist on site, without traveling

> Remote expert support is live help from a specialist who sees what the technician on site sees, via video on a tablet or AR glasses. Actarion combines it with digital work instructions: the expert can draw on the live image, point to the right part and turn the solution into a new instruction.

![Pen drawing of a technician's tablet linked to an expert's screen with live annotation for remote expert support](/img/illustraties/use-cases-remote-expert-support.svg)

## When does waiting for an expert cost you?

- A machine is down and the only person who knows it works another shift or another site.
- The OEM service engineer has a lead time of days.
- Phone calls go wrong: "the red cable on the left" is not specific enough.
- The same question is solved again and again because the answer is never written down.

## What do you measure?

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Time to resolution | Minutes from call to fixed |
| First-time fix rate | Share of issues fixed in one visit |
| Expert travel days | Avoided trips |
| Downtime per incident | Production impact |

## How does remote expert support work?

1. **Start a call from the step.** The technician calls for help from within the instruction they are running.
2. **Share the view.** The expert sees the live camera feed of the tablet or glasses.
3. **Annotate in AR.** The expert draws arrows and circles that stay on the right part, even when the camera moves.
4. **Solve and confirm.** The technician carries out the fix, the session is logged.
5. **Capture the knowledge.** The solution becomes a new step or tip in the instruction, so the next person needs no call.

## Hardware you need

- **AR glasses** for hands-free work while talking to the expert.
- **Tablet or smartphone** for quick calls and simple checks.
- The expert uses a laptop or desktop with a browser `[TE BEVESTIGEN: expert-client]`.

## How to start a pilot

Pick a team that often needs a specialist: second-line maintenance, field service or a site without its own expert. Measure time to resolution for a few weeks before and during the pilot. [Plan your pilot](/en/pilot/).

## FAQ

### Is this just a video call?
No. Annotations stay anchored on the object in 3D, and the session is linked to the job and its record.

### What about bandwidth?
A stable 4G or Wi-Fi connection is normally enough `[TE BEVESTIGEN: minimale bandbreedte]`.

### Can external parties like OEMs join?
Yes, with guest access for the session `[TE BEVESTIGEN: gasttoegang en beveiliging]`.
