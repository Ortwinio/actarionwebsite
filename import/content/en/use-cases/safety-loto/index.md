# Lockout tagout procedure: every isolation point, every time

> A lockout tagout procedure (LOTO) is the sequence for isolating all energy sources of a machine and securing them with a lock and tag before work starts. Actarion turns your machine-specific LOTO procedures into digital steps with AR: each isolation point is shown on the machine and every lock is confirmed and logged.

![Pen drawing of a main switch with padlock and tag and a tablet showing the lockout tagout procedure steps](/img/illustraties/use-cases-safety-loto.svg)

## Where does LOTO fail in practice?

- A second energy source, such as compressed air or stored pressure, is overlooked.
- The procedure on paper does not match the machine after a modification.
- The zero-energy check is skipped under time pressure.
- Contractors do not know your machines or your LOTO rules.

## How does a digital LOTO procedure work?

1. **Inventory energy sources** per machine: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, mechanical, thermal and stored energy.
2. **Build the procedure.** AI structures your existing LOTO sheets into steps with photos of each isolation point.
3. **Guide on the machine.** AR shows where each isolation point is, in the right order.
4. **Confirm each lock.** The technician confirms with a photo or scan per point.
5. **Verify zero energy.** The try-out or measurement is a mandatory step.
6. **Release in reverse.** Removal of locks follows the same guided sequence and is logged.

## Rules and standards

In the US, OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 requires energy control procedures, training and periodic inspections. In Europe, the Use of Work Equipment Directive 2009/104/EC requires that equipment can be isolated from energy sources. ISO 45001 asks for controlled procedures for hazardous work. A digital record helps you show compliance.

## Common mistakes in a pilot

- **Digitizing without checking the procedure.** Walk every isolation point on site first; outdated LOTO sheets are common.
- **Skipping the verification step.** The try-out (zero energy check) must be a mandatory step with confirmation.
- **Leaving out contractors.** They often carry the highest risk, so give them access to the same guided procedure.

## FAQ

### Does the digital procedure replace physical locks?
No. Locks and tags stay. The digital procedure guides and records the work.

### How do we keep procedures current after a modification?
The procedure is updated once in the studio and every device shows the new version.

### Can contractors use it?
Yes. Guided steps in their language help contractors follow your rules.
