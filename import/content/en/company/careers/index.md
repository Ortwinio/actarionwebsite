# Careers at Actarion: shape AR and AI on the shop floor

> Actarion is a small, entrepreneurial team that changes how factories and field teams work across Europe. You get responsibility early, room to experiment and short feedback loops. Current openings are an AR Developer in Germany, a Sales Engineer in the Netherlands and a Product Manager in Switzerland.

![Pen drawing: toolbox, laptop with code and a cyan screen, and AR glasses](/img/illustraties/company-careers.svg)

## Which roles are open?

| Role | Country | What you do | What you bring |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR Developer | Germany | Build AR applications in Unity or Unreal Engine | C# skills |
| Sales Engineer | Netherlands | Map prospects' processes and support pilots | Technical background, strong English |
| Product Manager | Switzerland | Set product priorities; turn pilot feedback into requirements | B2B or SaaS experience |

## What is it like to work at Actarion?

- Direct impact on how factories and field teams work.
- Responsibility early and room to experiment.
- Collaboration over hierarchy, open communication.
- The ambition to become a European reference for AR- and AI-guided work, together with DeepSight.
