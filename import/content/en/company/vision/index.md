# Our vision: guided work instead of documents

> Actarion's vision is to make complex industrial work simpler and smarter with AI and augmented reality. Operators get critical tasks right the first time, knowledge is captured and shared, and training happens inside the work itself. The vision is people first: technology removes friction so people can work more independently, not to replace them.

![A phone held up to process equipment shows AR steps and a confirmed check for future of work manufacturing](/images/company-vision-1600.webp)

## Why must the way we pass on knowledge change?

- Shortage of skilled workers and an ageing workforce.
- Rising demands on safety, quality and regulation.
- Pressure on costs, uptime and lead times.
- Static procedures, classroom courses and shadowing do not scale.

## What are the core ideas?

- **Guided work over documents:** step-by-step guidance, often overlaid on the equipment.
- **Context-aware support:** the right step at the right moment, in the right language.
- **Real-time capture:** checks, photos, measurements and notes recorded as work happens.
- **People first:** no fully automated factories, but more independent people.

## What does it mean per role?

| Role | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Operators | Clear, visual steps instead of long text |
| Experts | Their know-how is preserved and reused |
| Management | Visibility into skills, compliance and performance |

## Where are we heading?

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Pilots | Standard procedures and training |
| Isolated plants | Connected networks that share best practices, with room for local adaptation |
| Reactive support | Predictive guidance based on execution and sensor data |

## What does responsible innovation mean to us?

Solutions must be transparent, safe, measurable (errors, downtime, training time) and sustainable, meaning your own team can maintain them.
