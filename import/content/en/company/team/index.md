# The Actarion team: operations and digital experience

> The Actarion team combines shop-floor operations with digital business experience. Mark Dercksen leads DACH, Jack Kessels leads Benelux and founder Ortwin Verreck sits on the advisory board. Together they help industrial companies choose the right use cases, run pilots with measurable results and roll out AR work instructions across sites.

![Pen drawing: three silhouettes of the team below a tablet with a cyan step card](/img/illustraties/company-team.svg)

## Who leads Actarion?

| Name | Role | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Mark Dercksen | Managing Partner DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) | Customer and partner networks in DACH; aligning AR and AI projects with local operations and regulations |
| Jack Kessels | Managing Partner Benelux (Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg) | High-impact AR and AI use cases; pilot and roll-out roadmaps |
| Ortwin Verreck | Founder & Advisory Board | Digital business models, AI and AR adoption, partner ecosystems |

## What experience does the team bring?

- **Mark Dercksen:** commercial, operational and transformation roles in international B2B companies; sales leadership, key account management, operational excellence and change management.
- **Jack Kessels:** operational leadership in food, FMCG and interim transformation; improvement programs with measurable results on the shop floor.
- **Ortwin Verreck:** built and scaled digital businesses in Europe; led and advised companies in digital marketing, AI and SaaS.
