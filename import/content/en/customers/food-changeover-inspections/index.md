# Food case: AR-guided changeovers and inspections

> A North-American food producer used AR work instructions to train new operators, guide line changeovers and standardize pre-inspections. On the DeepSight platform it built 105 training courses that 300 employees can use, so experienced staff spend less time coaching. Actarion delivers this approach to European food plants.

![A phone held up to a robotic assembly line shows AR steps and a confirmed check for ar changeover food production](/images/customers-food-changeover-inspections-1600.webp)

*Case realised with technology partner DeepSight. Figures as published by DeepSight; customer anonymised.*

## At a glance

- **105** training courses built
- **300** employees can use the new training
- Modules: 3D instructions, knowledge capture, skills management

## Why did training keep pulling experts off the line?

- High turnover meant a constant stream of new hires to train.
- Experienced operators left their own work to coach newcomers.
- Operators depended on trainers to get through paper instructions.

## Starting situation

The producer faced high staff turnover and a workforce with limited formal education. New hires needed training all the time. Experienced workers coached them, which took them away from production.

## Approach

1. Capture expert know-how for training, line changeovers and pre-inspections.
2. Build visual, step-by-step instructions in 3D.
3. Let operators follow the steps on their own at the line.
4. Track competence per operator in the skills management module.
5. Update instructions centrally when a product or line changes.

## Results

| Result | Figure / effect |
|---|---|
| Training courses built | 105 |
| Employees who can use the training | 300 |
| Coaching load on experts | Lower: operators follow the instructions on their own |

## FAQ

### Do operators need much IT experience?
No. Instructions are visual and short, and work on a familiar tablet or phone.

### Does this help with food safety audits?
Yes. Each pre-inspection is logged with time and confirmations, which supports HACCP records.

### How long do you need to build a changeover instruction?
That depends on the line. With AI authoring you start from your existing SOP, not from a blank page.
