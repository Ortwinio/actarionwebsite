# Mining case: AR training and maintenance across sites

> A global mining company used AR work instructions to standardize training, maintenance and pre-inspections across its mine sites. Built on the DeepSight platform, the program delivered 40 training courses over three sites, with 3D instructions, remote assistance and operations traceability. Actarion delivers this approach to European operations.

![A technician holds an iPad up to process equipment; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for ar training mining](/images/customers-mining-training-maintenance-1600.webp)

*Case realised with technology partner DeepSight. Figures as published by DeepSight; customer anonymised.*

## At a glance

- **40** training courses built on the platform
- **3** sites served with the same standard
- Modules: 3D instructions, remote assistance, operations traceability

## Why was training across mine sites so hard?

- Each acquired mine worked its own way, so best practices did not travel.
- Tough training conditions and language barriers slowed new hires down.
- High staff turnover raised safety concerns.

## Starting situation

The company grew fast by acquiring mines. Most sites operated independently, each with its own procedures. Training happened in difficult conditions and across languages, while turnover stayed high.

## Approach

1. Select the critical tasks: training, maintenance, pre-inspections.
2. Turn existing procedures into 3D, step-by-step instructions.
3. Roll out the same instructions to three sites.
4. Add remote assistance so experts can support crews at a distance.
5. Log every execution for operations traceability.

## Results

| Training courses built | 40 |
| Sites served | 3 |

## FAQ

### Does AR training work in harsh environments?
Yes. Crews can use rugged tablets or smartphones; AR glasses are optional for hands-free tasks.

### How do you share best practices between sites?
You publish one approved instruction centrally and push it to every site, with local language versions.

### Can remote experts see what the technician sees?
Yes. Remote assistance lets an expert join the task live and guide the technician step by step.
