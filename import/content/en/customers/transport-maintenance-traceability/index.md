# Transport case: AR maintenance training with traceability

> A transport and logistics company used AR work instructions to train hundreds of new hires in preventive maintenance and to document every job. On the DeepSight platform it built 20 training courses for two sites, with evaluations, skills management and remote assistance. Actarion delivers this approach to fleet and logistics operations in Europe.

![A technician holds an iPad up to process equipment; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for preventive maintenance training transport](/images/customers-transport-maintenance-traceability-1600.webp)

*Case realised with technology partner DeepSight. Figures as published by DeepSight; customer anonymised.*

## At a glance

- **20** training courses built
- **2** sites served
- Modules: 3D instructions, evaluations, skills management

## Why was maintenance training a bottleneck during growth?

- Rapid growth brought hundreds of new hires at once.
- Experienced technicians were scarce.
- Training and maintenance records were not standardized.

## Starting situation

The company grew quickly and hired hundreds of people who needed preventive maintenance skills fast. Experienced staff were hard to find. Training had to be standardized and maintenance documented.

## Approach

1. Standardize the preventive maintenance and inspection routines.
2. Build step-by-step 3D instructions for each routine.
3. Evaluate new hires on the instructions and record results.
4. Add remote assistance for unusual faults.
5. Log each job for traceability.

## Results

## FAQ

### Can technicians use this in the workshop and on the road?
Yes. Instructions run on phones and tablets, so they work wherever the vehicle is.

### How does traceability work?
Each executed step is logged with time, user and confirmation, so you can show what was done on which vehicle.

### How do you onboard many hires at once?
Everyone follows the same instructions and evaluations, and the skills matrix shows who is ready to work alone.
