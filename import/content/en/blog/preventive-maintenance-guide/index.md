# Preventive Maintenance: How to Build a Plan That Gets Done

> Preventive maintenance is maintenance carried out on a fixed schedule or usage interval, before equipment fails. It reduces unplanned downtime and extends asset life. The plan only works if every task is done the same way and recorded. Actarion guides technicians through each PM task on a tablet or AR glasses and logs every check automatically.

## Why does preventive maintenance slip?

- PM tasks get postponed when production is busy, then the breakdown comes anyway.
- The checklist says "inspect belt" and every technician inspects something different.
- Experienced technicians retire, and their routines leave with them.
- Signed checklists exist, but you cannot see what was actually measured.

## What is preventive maintenance?

Preventive maintenance (PM) is planned work that keeps equipment in good condition before it fails. It sits between reactive maintenance (fix it when it breaks) and predictive maintenance (act on condition data such as vibration or temperature).

| Strategy | Trigger | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reactive | Failure | No planning effort | Unplanned downtime, safety risk |
| Preventive, time-based | Calendar interval | Simple to plan | May replace parts too early |
| Preventive, usage-based | Operating hours or cycles | Better fit to wear | Needs counters |
| Predictive / condition-based | Sensor data | Act only when needed | Needs sensors and analysis |

Most plants combine them. Preventive maintenance remains the backbone for most assets.

## How do you set up a preventive maintenance plan in 6 steps?

1. **List your assets and rank them by criticality.** What happens to safety, quality and output if this asset fails?
2. **Collect the requirements.** Manufacturer manuals, legal inspections and your own failure history.
3. **Define tasks and intervals.** Per asset: what, how often, by whom and how long it takes.
4. **Write each task as a step-by-step checklist.** With values, tolerances and photos. Start with the [preventive maintenance checklist](/en/resources/templates/preventive-maintenance-checklist/).
5. **Schedule it.** In your CMMS or maintenance planning, aligned with production windows.
6. **Record, review and adjust.** Track completion and findings. Adjust intervals based on what you find.

## Example

Monthly PM for a centrifugal pump in a water treatment plant:

1. Check for leaks at the mechanical seal. *Photo.*
2. Measure bearing temperature with an IR thermometer. *Value, limit `[TE BEVESTIGEN: per asset]`.*
3. Listen for abnormal noise and check vibration by hand or meter.
4. Check coupling guard is in place and secured.
5. Check oil level in the sight glass. Top up if below the mark.
6. Record findings and create a work order for any deviation.

## Common mistakes

- **Vague tasks.** "Check pump" is not a task. Name the measurement and the limit.
- **Intervals copied from the manual and never reviewed.**
- **No feedback from findings.** If nothing is ever found, the interval may be too short.
- **Paper checklists that are signed in bulk** at the end of the week.

## How do you measure success?

Measure before you change anything. Record a baseline for two to four weeks, then compare after the rollout using the same definitions. These KPIs are the most useful:

| KPI | What it tells you | How you measure it |
|---|---|---|
| PM compliance | Whether planned tasks happen on time | Share of PM tasks completed in their window |
| Unplanned downtime | Whether PM prevents failures | Hours of unplanned stops per month |
| Task completeness | Whether every step is done | Share of PM records with all checks and readings |
| Repeat failures | Whether root causes are fixed | Failures on the same asset within 30 days |

Keep the list short: three KPIs that the team leader reviews every week beat ten that nobody looks at. Agree on the definitions with quality and finance up front, so the results hold up in a business case.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between preventive and predictive maintenance?
Preventive maintenance follows a fixed time or usage interval. Predictive maintenance acts on measured condition, such as vibration or temperature trends.

### How often should preventive maintenance be done?
It depends on the asset, the manufacturer's advice, legal requirements and your failure history. Review intervals regularly.

### Does preventive maintenance need a CMMS?
Not to start, but it helps with scheduling and history. Digital checklists can link to it.

### Who should do preventive maintenance tasks?
Technicians for complex tasks; operators can do simple checks and cleaning, often as part of autonomous maintenance in TPM.
