# Connected worker platform: one place for instructions, skills, experts

> A connected worker platform links frontline workers to the instructions, people and data they need while they work. Actarion's connected worker platform combines AI-generated work instructions, AR guidance on tablet or AR glasses, a skills matrix, remote expert support and an audit trail, so every operator works the right way.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a control room; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for connected worker platform](/images/solutions-connected-worker-platform-1600.webp)

## Why do frontline workers still work disconnected?

- Instructions, training records and machine data live in separate systems, or on paper.
- When something goes wrong, the operator waits for an expert who is on another shift or site.
- Team leaders do not know who is qualified for which task until a problem occurs.
- Management sees output figures, but not how the work is actually done.

## What is a connected worker platform?

A connected worker platform is software that gives frontline workers digital access to instructions, checklists, colleagues and data at the point of work. A connected worker is an operator, technician or inspector who works with that digital support instead of paper and memory.

The platform also works the other way. It sends data from the shop floor back to the office: who did which task, how long it took and where deviations happened.

## How does Actarion's connected worker platform work?

1. **Digitize the knowledge.** AI turns SOPs and work instructions into digital steps. See [AI authoring](/en/platform/ai-authoring/).
2. **Assign the work.** Team leaders link instructions to roles and qualifications in the skills matrix.
3. **Guide at the point of work.** Operators follow the steps with AR on a tablet or AR glasses. See [AR guidance](/en/platform/ar-guidance/).
4. **Get help live.** A remote expert looks along through the operator's camera and annotates the view. See [remote expert support](/en/use-cases/remote-expert-support/).
5. **Measure and improve.** Execution data flows into dashboards and the audit trail. See [analytics and audit trail](/en/platform/analytics-audit-trail/).

## Key features

## How do you implement a connected worker platform?

1. **Start with one team** and one process where errors or downtime hurt most.
2. **Agree on KPIs and a baseline,** for example time to competence or unplanned downtime.
3. **Digitize the core instructions** and set up the skills matrix for that team.
4. **Run the pilot** with tablets first. Add AR glasses where hands-free work matters. See [start a pilot](/en/pilot/).
5. **Scale** to more teams, sites and use cases, and train internal champions.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a platform before choosing use cases.** Start from two or three concrete tasks, not from a feature list.
- **Connecting everything at once.** Integrations with MES, ERP or CMMS can follow once the pilot proves value.
- **Forgetting the frontline.** Involve operators early; they decide whether the tool is used.
- **No owner for content.** Without authors and a review cycle, instructions go stale within months.

## FAQ

### What is a connected worker?
A connected worker is a frontline employee who uses digital tools such as tablets, AR glasses and apps to access instructions, help and data during work.

### Is a connected worker platform the same as an MES?
No. An MES controls production orders and machines. A connected worker platform supports the people doing the work. The two can complement each other. [TE BEVESTIGEN: MES integrations]

### Do operators need AR glasses?
No. Most teams start on tablets. AR glasses add value for hands-free tasks such as maintenance and assembly.
