# Digital checklists for manufacturing: no skipped checks, with proof

> A digital checklist in manufacturing replaces the paper form with a checklist on a tablet or AR glasses that enforces every check, captures photos and measurements and timestamps each item. Actarion builds digital checklists from your existing forms with AI and links them to work instructions and an audit trail.

![Pen drawing: a tablet with a digital checklist, ticked steps and photo evidence, next to a paper clipboard](/img/illustraties/solutions-digital-checklists.svg)

## What goes wrong with paper checklists?

- Boxes are ticked at the end of the shift, not during the check.
- Illegible notes and missing values make the checklist useless for analysis.
- Nobody sees a deviation until someone types up the forms.
- Paper archives make audits slow and stressful.

## What is a digital checklist?

A digital checklist is a list of checks that an operator completes on a device, item by item. Each item can require a value, a photo or a signature before the next one opens. The result is stored automatically with time, user and location.

In manufacturing, digital checklists are used for start-up checks, line clearance, quality inspections, safety rounds and maintenance.

## Which checklists should you digitize first?

Start where a missed check costs the most. This table helps you prioritize:

| Checklist | Typical risk on paper | Why digital helps first |
|---|---|---|
| Start-up and line clearance | Product mix-ups, wrong labels | Mandatory steps and photo evidence per batch |
| Changeover (SMED) | Long stops, start-up scrap | Fixed sequence, settings shown per product |
| Preventive maintenance | Skipped tasks, missing readings | Readings with limits, overdue tasks visible |
| Quality inspection | Values written down later | Values checked against tolerance on entry |
| Safety and LOTO | Isolation points missed | Each point confirmed before work starts |

## How do you implement digital checklists?

1. **Select one checklist** with a clear problem, such as audit findings or recurring deviations.
2. **Clean it up.** Remove checks nobody uses and add tolerances, photos and the action on deviation.
3. **Convert it with AI** and link each check to the instruction that explains how to do it.
4. **Test on the line** with two or three operators and the team leader, then adjust.
5. **Go live and measure** completeness, deviations and time per round against your paper baseline.
6. **Scale** to the next checklists and train your own authors.

## Common mistakes

- **Copying the paper form one-to-one.** You digitize the old gaps along with it.
- **Too many mandatory fields.** Operators click through without looking. Make only the critical checks mandatory.
- **No owner for deviations.** A flagged deviation without follow-up is just a digital tick mark.
- **Forgetting the device at the workstation.** Decide where the tablet hangs and how it is charged before go-live.
