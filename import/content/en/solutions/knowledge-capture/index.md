# Knowledge capture: keep expert know-how when people leave

> Knowledge capture is the process of recording how experienced employees do their work, so others can repeat it. Actarion captures expert know-how on video or during the task, turns it into AI-generated, step-by-step work instructions with AR, and makes it available to every operator, with a record of every execution.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a production hall; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for knowledge capture](/images/solutions-knowledge-capture-1600.webp)

## Why is knowledge walking out of your plant?

- Experienced technicians retire, and much of what they know was never written down.
- Rare tasks, such as an annual overhaul, depend on one or two people.
- New colleagues ask the same questions, and experts lose time answering them.
- When the expert is on leave, a breakdown takes longer to fix.

## What is knowledge capture?

Knowledge capture is recording tacit, experience-based knowledge (often called tribal knowledge) and making it explicit and reusable. On the shop floor that means capturing not just what an expert does, but also the tips, checks and order that make the job go right.

Knowledge transfer is the next step: getting that knowledge to the people who need it, at the moment they need it.

## How does knowledge capture work with Actarion?

1. **Record the expert.** The expert performs the task while it is filmed on a phone, tablet or AR glasses, and explains as they go.
2. **Generate instructions with AI.** AI turns the recording into structured steps with images and checks. See [AI authoring](/en/platform/ai-authoring/).
3. **Review together.** The expert checks the result and adds tips and warnings.
4. **Share with AR.** Other operators follow the instruction with AR guidance. See [AR guidance](/en/platform/ar-guidance/).
5. **Keep improving.** Execution data shows where people get stuck, so you can refine the instruction. See [analytics and audit trail](/en/platform/analytics-audit-trail/).

## Which knowledge should you capture first?

Not all knowledge is equally at risk. Score each task on three questions and start with the highest total:

| Question | Low risk | High risk |
|---|---|---|
| How many people can do this task? | Many | One or two |
| When does the expert leave or retire? | No date known | Within two years |
| What happens if it goes wrong? | Small delay | Long downtime, safety or quality incident |

## How do you implement knowledge capture?

1. **Make a risk list** of critical tasks per expert, together with the team leaders.
2. **Plan recording sessions** during real work, such as a planned overhaul or a rare changeover.
3. **Record and convert.** The expert records with a phone, tablet or AR glasses; AI turns it into steps.
4. **Review with the expert** and add tips, warnings and tolerances per step.
5. **Let a colleague repeat the task** with AR guidance while the expert watches, and sign off.
6. **Measure and extend:** coverage of the risk list, trained backups per task and calls to the expert.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting until the farewell party.** Capture starts a year or more before someone leaves, not in their last week.
- **Long, unedited videos.** Nobody watches 40 minutes at the machine. Cut knowledge into short, findable steps.
- **Capturing without a successor.** Knowledge only transfers when someone actually performs the task and is signed off.
