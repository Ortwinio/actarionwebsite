# SOP software: from paper SOP to digital SOP that people follow

> SOP software is a system to write, approve, distribute and execute standard operating procedures digitally. Actarion's SOP software uses an AI SOP generator to convert your existing documents into digital SOPs, guides operators step by step with AR on tablet or AR glasses, and logs every execution for your audits.

![Pen drawing from above: a stack of paper SOPs converted by AI into digital step cards on a tablet](/img/illustraties/solutions-sop-software.svg)

## Why do SOPs on paper cause problems?

- Writing and updating SOPs takes your engineers days. So updates are postponed.
- Old versions keep circulating on the shop floor. People follow what they find.
- An SOP written for an auditor is not an instruction an operator can follow at the machine.
- You can show that an SOP exists, but not that it was followed.

## What is SOP software?

An SOP, short for standard operating procedure, describes how a recurring task must be done to get a consistent, safe and compliant result. SOP software manages the full life cycle of those procedures: writing, review, approval, distribution, training and execution.

A digital SOP is not a PDF in a shared folder. It is a structured procedure with steps, images and checks that operators open on a device at the point of work. An AI SOP generator speeds up the writing: it turns existing documents, photos or a recorded walkthrough into a draft SOP.

## How does Actarion's SOP software work?

1. **Collect your SOPs.** Upload Word files, PDFs, scans or a video of an expert doing the job.
2. **Generate with AI.** The AI SOP generator structures the content into steps, adds images and proposes checks and warnings. See [AI authoring](/en/platform/ai-authoring/).
3. **Review and approve.** Process owners and quality edit and approve. Every change creates a new version with a record of who approved it.
4. **Publish to the floor.** Operators always see the current version, on a tablet or AR glasses. See [AR guidance](/en/platform/ar-guidance/).
5. **Execute and log.** Each execution records time, confirmations, measurements and deviations. See [analytics and audit trail](/en/platform/analytics-audit-trail/).

## Common mistakes

- **Moving the PDF chaos into new software.** Clean up and merge duplicate SOPs before you convert them.
- **No clear owner per SOP.** Every procedure needs a process owner who approves changes and review dates.
- **Separating the SOP from the shop floor.** If operators still work from a printout, the software only helps the document controller.
- **Skipping read-and-understood.** Link each new version to sign-off by the people who perform it.
