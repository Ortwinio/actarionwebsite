# Digital work instructions: fewer errors, every step on record

> Digital work instructions are step-by-step procedures shown on a tablet or AR glasses instead of paper, with images, video and mandatory checks. Actarion uses AI to turn your existing SOPs into digital work instructions, guides operators with AR overlays and logs every execution as audit evidence.

![Pen drawing from above: a flange with bolts, an AR marker on the next bolt and a tablet showing digital work instructions](/img/illustraties/solutions-digital-work-instructions.svg)

## Why do paper work instructions fail on the shop floor?

- Binders and PDFs are out of date the day they are printed. Nobody knows which version is valid.
- Text-heavy instructions are hard to follow at the machine. Operators skip steps or ask a colleague.
- A missed step means rework, scrap or a safety incident. You only find out afterwards.
- During an audit you cannot prove who did what, when and in which order.

## What are digital work instructions?

Digital work instructions are procedures that guide an operator step by step on a screen, with one action per step. Each step combines short text with photos, video or 3D. Checks, measurements and sign-offs are built in.

Visual work instructions put the image first and the text second. AR work instructions go one step further: the instruction appears as an overlay on the real machine, so the operator sees exactly which bolt, valve or connector is next.

Work instruction software is the system that creates, manages and runs these instructions. It handles versions, approvals, users and the data from every execution.

## Paper vs. digital vs. digital with AR

| Criterion | Paper / PDF | Digital work instructions | Digital + AR (Actarion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to date | Often outdated, many copies | One central version | One central version |
| Ease of use at the machine | Text-heavy, hands needed | Visual, step by step | Overlay on the machine, hands-free on glasses |
| Error prevention | None | Mandatory checks | Checks plus visual location of each action |
| Proof of execution | Signature on paper | Digital log | Digital log with photos and time per step |
| Authoring effort | Manual writing | Manual or template-based | AI-generated from existing SOPs |
| Onboarding | Shadowing a colleague | Self-guided | Self-guided with AR context |

## How do you implement digital work instructions?

Start small and measure.

1. **Pick one process** with a clear pain: errors, long changeovers or long onboarding.
2. **Set a baseline** for the KPIs you want to improve, such as error rate or minutes per changeover.
3. **Digitize two to five instructions** with AI authoring and review them with your experts.
4. **Run a pilot** on one line or team. See [start a pilot](/en/pilot/).
5. **Evaluate and scale** to more lines, sites and languages.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between digital and AR work instructions?
Digital work instructions show the steps on a screen. AR work instructions place the step as an overlay on the real equipment, so the operator sees where to act.
