# Digital work instructions with AI and AR, audit-ready from day one

> Actarion turns your existing SOPs and work instructions into digital, step-by-step work instructions using AI. Operators follow them with AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses. Every execution is logged with time, confirmations and deviations, so you get faster onboarding, fewer errors and audit evidence without extra paperwork.

![Pen drawing: a paper SOP becomes a digital work instruction on a tablet and AR glasses, with execution data flowing to a dashboard](/img/illustraties/home.svg)

## Does this sound familiar on your shop floor?

- New operators need weeks before they can run a line on their own, and your best people spend that time training instead of producing.
- Work instructions live in binders and PDFs that nobody opens. Errors, rework and scrap follow.
- Your most experienced technicians retire, and their know-how leaves with them.
- Every audit means chasing signatures and proving that people followed the procedure.

## How do digital work instructions with AI and AR work?

Actarion connects three steps: **SOP → AI → AR + data**.

1. **Upload your SOP.** Bring the documents you already have: Word, PDF, photos or a recorded walkthrough. AI authoring turns them into structured, step-by-step instructions with images and checks. Your experts review and approve.
2. **Guide the operator with AR.** The operator follows each step on a tablet or AR glasses such as Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2 or Android XR. The overlay shows exactly where to act on the machine.
3. **Capture the data.** Every execution records time per step, confirmations, photos and deviations. You get an audit trail and insights to improve the process.

## FAQ

### What are digital work instructions?
Digital work instructions are step-by-step procedures shown on a tablet, phone or AR glasses instead of on paper. They combine text, images, video and checks, and they record each execution.

### Do I need AR glasses to get started?
No. Most teams start on a tablet. You can add AR glasses such as Apple Vision Pro or HoloLens 2 later for hands-free work.
