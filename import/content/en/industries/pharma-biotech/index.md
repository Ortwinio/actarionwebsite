# GMP work instructions for pharma and biotech, audit-ready by design

> GMP work instructions describe exactly how operators execute production steps under Good Manufacturing Practice. Actarion converts your SOPs into guided digital steps with AR support for batch preparation, line clearance and changeovers. Every execution is recorded with time, user and deviations, which supports data integrity and inspections.

![Pen drawing of a stainless reactor, vials and a tablet with a batch checklist for GMP work instructions](/img/illustraties/industries-pharma-biotech.svg)

## What keeps pharma operations managers awake?

- Documentation errors in batch records cause deviations and delays in release.
- Line clearance is a ticked box, and a mix-up still happens.
- Training records must prove that each operator was qualified on the current SOP version.
- Inspectors look closely at data integrity: who did what, when, and was it changed?

## Which regulations apply?

- **EU GMP (EudraLex Volume 4).** Chapter 4 covers documentation, Chapter 5 production, including checks that equipment and work areas are clear of previous products before a new operation.
- **EU GMP Annex 11** sets requirements for computerised systems, such as validation, audit trails and access control.
- **21 CFR Part 11** (US FDA) covers electronic records and electronic signatures.
- **ALCOA+** principles: data must be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring and available.

## How do you start a pilot in pharma and biotech?

1. **Pick one GMP procedure** with many deviations or long training time, such as line clearance or a cleaning procedure.
2. **Involve QA from day one.** Agree how the digital instruction relates to the controlled SOP and how changes are approved.
3. **Plan validation** in proportion to the risk, in line with your computerized systems approach (for example GAMP 5).
4. **Convert and review:** AI creates the steps, the process owner and QA approve them.
5. **Measure** deviations, right-first-time batch records and time to qualification. Use the results for the decision to scale.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.
