# Digital work instructions for every industry with critical procedures

> Digital work instructions help any industry where people carry out critical, repeated procedures and must prove they did it right. Actarion serves manufacturing, pharma and biotech, food and beverage, energy and utilities, aerospace MRO, machine builders and field service. The core is the same: SOP to AI to AR, with an audit trail.

![Pen drawing of a factory, reactor, power pylon and wind turbine around a tablet showing digital work instructions](/img/illustraties/industries.svg)

## What do these industries have in common?

- Experienced staff retire faster than new staff can be trained.
- Procedures are strict, and auditors want proof of execution.
- Errors cost downtime, scrap, recalls or safety incidents.
- Teams are mixed: shifts, temp staff, contractors and several languages.

## Which industry, which focus?

| Industry | Main challenges | Key standards | Typical first use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Manufacturing](/en/industries/manufacturing/) | Variants, changeovers, OEE | ISO 9001, ISO 45001 | Changeover, assembly |
| [Pharma & biotech](/en/industries/pharma-biotech/) | GMP, data integrity, line clearance | EU GMP, Annex 11, 21 CFR Part 11 | Batch preparation, line clearance |
| [Food & beverage](/en/industries/food-beverage/) | Changeovers, cleaning, food safety | HACCP, ISO 22000 | Changeover, CIP cleaning |
| [Energy & utilities](/en/industries/energy-utilities/) | Safety, field work, shutdowns | ISO 45001, EN 50110-1 | LOTO, maintenance |
| [Aerospace MRO](/en/industries/aerospace-mro/) | Traceability, human factors | EASA Part-145 | Maintenance tasks |
| [Machine builders](/en/industries/machine-builders/) | Service, instructions with machines | Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 | Service instructions |
| [Field service](/en/industries/field-service/) | Remote sites, first-time fix | ISO 9001 | Checklists, remote support |
