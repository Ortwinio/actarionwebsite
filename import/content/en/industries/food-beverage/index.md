# Work instructions for food and beverage: fast changeovers, safe food

> Food and beverage plants run many short batches, frequent changeovers and strict cleaning routines. Actarion turns your changeover, cleaning and inspection procedures into digital work instructions with AR guidance. Operators follow the same steps on every shift, and every execution is recorded as evidence for HACCP and customer audits.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a conveyor line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for food and beverage work instructions](/images/industries-food-beverage-1600.webp)

## What challenges do food plants face?

- Many SKUs and changeovers per day eat into line capacity.
- Allergen changeovers and cleaning must be flawless.
- Seasonal and temp staff need to work safely on day one.
- Auditors want complete records of CCP monitoring and cleaning.

## Which standards apply?

- **HACCP:** Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 (Article 5) requires food businesses to apply procedures based on HACCP principles, following the Codex Alimentarius General Principles of Food Hygiene.
- **ISO 22000** and certification schemes such as FSSC 22000, BRCGS and IFS require documented procedures and records.
- **CIP cleaning** (cleaning in place) must be validated and executed consistently, with records of each cycle.

## How do you start a pilot in food and beverage?

1. **Pick one line and one routine,** for example a changeover with allergen cleaning or a CCP check, where errors are costly.
2. **Align with your HACCP team** so critical control points become mandatory steps with recorded values.
3. **Convert the procedure with AI,** add photos of the real line and have the line lead review each step.
4. **Check hygiene and device rules:** which tablet case, where it is mounted and how it is cleaned.
5. **Measure** changeover time, allergen-related deviations and record completeness over several weeks. Then roll out to the next line.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.

## FAQ

### Do the devices work in wet areas?
Use rugged tablets with a suitable IP rating, or AR glasses where washdown allows `[TE BEVESTIGEN: hardware voor natte zones]`.

### Can it replace our HACCP paperwork?
It can replace paper records for the steps it guides. Your HACCP plan itself stays your responsibility.

### Where should we start?
On the line with the most changeovers or the most cleaning deviations.
