# Digital work instructions for manufacturing: fewer errors, higher OEE

> Digital work instructions in manufacturing replace binders and PDFs with step-by-step guidance at the workstation. Actarion uses AI to convert your SOPs and assembly instructions, and AR to show each step on the machine. The result: faster changeovers, fewer assembly errors, quicker onboarding and data that helps raise OEE.

![A phone held up to a robotic assembly line shows AR steps and a confirmed check for digital work instructions manufacturing](/images/industries-manufacturing-1600.webp)

## What challenges do manufacturers face?

- More variants and smaller batches, so more changeovers and more chances for error.
- OEE losses from long, unpredictable changeovers and minor stoppages.
- Experienced operators leaving, and new staff who need months to learn.
- Instructions that lag behind engineering changes.

## Which standards apply?

- **ISO 9001** asks for controlled documented information (clause 7.5) and proof of competence (clause 7.2). Digital instructions with version control and a skills matrix support both.
- **ISO 45001** requires controls for hazardous tasks, such as energy isolation during maintenance.
- **IATF 16949** for automotive suppliers adds strict requirements on work instructions at the workstation.

## How do you start a pilot in manufacturing?

1. **Pick one line and two or three tasks** with visible pain: a frequent changeover, an error-prone assembly step or a long onboarding.
2. **Convert the existing SOPs** with AI and have the line lead check the steps at the machine.
3. **Train a small group** of operators and one team leader as key users.
4. **Measure** changeover time, first-time-right rate and time to competence against a baseline of several weeks.
5. **Decide on scale-up** with the data, and train your own authors for the next lines.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.

## FAQ

### Do we need to connect to MES or ERP?
Not for a pilot. Integrations can follow when you scale `[TE BEVESTIGEN: MES/ERP-koppelingen]`.

### Will operators accept it?
Acceptance is highest when operators help build the instructions. We involve them from the start of the pilot.

### Which line should we start with?
The line with the most changeovers or the most errors, so the effect is easy to measure.
