# Field service work instructions: fixed right on the first visit

> Field service work instructions guide technicians through installation, maintenance and repair at the customer's site. Actarion gives them digital checklists and step-by-step AR guidance on a tablet or glasses, plus remote support from an expert. The result: more first-time fixes, fewer return visits and a complete job report.

![A phone held up to a high-voltage line shows AR steps and a confirmed check for field service work instructions](/images/industries-field-service-1600.webp)

## What challenges do field service teams face?

- Technicians work alone and meet equipment they rarely see.
- Return visits because a part, a step or a measurement was missed.
- Job reports are incomplete or written up at the end of the day.
- Senior technicians are overloaded with phone calls from junior colleagues.

## Which standards apply?

- **ISO 9001** for documented procedures and records of service delivery.
- **ISO 45001** for safe work, including lone working and isolation.
- Sector rules depending on equipment, such as **EN 50110-1** for electrical installations or F-gas rules for cooling equipment.

## How do you start a pilot in field service?

1. **Pick one asset type** that causes the most repeat visits or long calls to senior engineers.
2. **Collect what exists:** service manuals, checklists, photos and short videos from your best engineer doing the job.
3. **Convert it with AI** into a guided procedure with mandatory photos and readings at the steps that cause repeat visits.
4. **Prepare the field conditions:** load instructions before the visit, test the connection for remote support and agree who answers expert calls.
5. **Measure** first-time-fix rate, report completeness and time on site against a baseline. Extend to the next asset type when the results hold.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.

Instructions can be loaded in advance; remote support needs a mobile connection `[TE BEVESTIGEN: offline-modus]`.

### Can subcontractors use it?
Yes, with their own access and the same guided steps.

Yes. For rare jobs, remote support helps most, and a good session can be saved as a new instruction.
