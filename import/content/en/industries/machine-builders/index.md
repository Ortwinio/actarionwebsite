# Digital service instructions for machine builders and OEMs

> Machine builders can ship digital service and operating instructions with every machine. Actarion turns your manuals into step-by-step instructions with AR guidance, for your own service engineers and for your customers' operators. Remote support connects them to your experts, and the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 explicitly allows digital instructions.

![Pen drawing of a machine with built-in screen, service case and the machine builder's console for digital service instructions](/img/illustraties/industries-machine-builders.svg)

## What challenges do machine builders face?

- Service engineers are scarce and spend days traveling.
- Customers misuse the machine or skip maintenance, and the warranty claim lands on your desk.
- Thick manuals in many languages that nobody reads.
- Installation and commissioning depend on a few experienced people.

## What does the Machinery Regulation say?

- **Regulation (EU) 2023/1230** replaces the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and applies from 20 January 2027.
- Instructions for use may be supplied in **digital format**. At the customer's request at the time of purchase, the manufacturer must provide a paper version free of charge.
- Digital instructions must be accessible for the expected lifetime of the machine, and for at least ten years after it is placed on the market.
- For machinery intended for non-professional users, safety information must still be supplied on paper.

Check the final text and harmonized standards with your compliance advisor.

## How do you start a pilot in machine building?

1. **Pick one machine type** with many service calls or long commissioning times.
2. **Start from the existing manual** and the knowledge of your best service engineer. AI turns both into guided steps.
3. **Decide who uses it:** your own engineers, the customer's operators or both. Access rights follow from that.
4. **Link remote support** to the instruction so your experts can see the machine without travelling.
5. **Measure** commissioning time, first-time-fix rate and calls to the service desk for that machine type before you scale.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.
