# Work instructions for energy and utilities: safe in the field

> Energy and utility companies run critical assets across many sites, often with small crews. Actarion gives field technicians digital work instructions with AR guidance for maintenance, isolation and inspections. Every job is logged, so safety procedures are followed and documented, also at remote sites and during shutdowns.

![A technician holds an iPad up to a high-voltage line; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for energy utilities work instructions](/images/industries-energy-utilities-1600.webp)

## What challenges does the sector face?

- An aging workforce and a growing asset base, from grids to renewables.
- High-risk work: high voltage, pressure, heights and confined spaces.
- Technicians work alone at remote sites, far from the expert.
- Shutdowns and outages with tight windows and many contractors.

## Which standards apply?

- **ISO 45001** for occupational health and safety management.
- **EN 50110-1** for operation of and work on electrical installations, including the five safety rules before work on de-energized parts.
- **ISO 55001** for asset management, which asks for documented, consistent maintenance.
- National rules such as OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 (US) for energy control.

## How do you start a pilot in energy and utilities?

1. **Pick one recurring field task,** such as a substation inspection, a switching procedure or a pump station check, with clear safety and quality risks.
2. **Agree on the safety rules first.** Switching and isolation steps must match your own operating rules and permit-to-work system.
3. **Convert the existing procedure** with AI and have an experienced engineer check each step on site, with photos of the real asset.
4. **Test offline use and connectivity** at remote locations before go-live. Decide which steps need a signal and which do not.
5. **Measure** completeness of inspection records, repeat visits and time to competence for new engineers, then decide on the next asset type.

The duration of a first pilot depends on scope and site `[TE BEVESTIGEN: standaard pilotduur]`. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/) for the full method.
