# FAQ about AI and AR work instructions

> AI- and AR-guided work instructions are digital, step-by-step instructions that AI creates from your existing SOPs and that operators follow with AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses. Actarion, DeepSight's European partner, implements them in Benelux, DACH and the Nordics. Below are answers on devices, use cases, audits, implementation and pricing.

## What do you need to know about Actarion?

### What does Actarion do?
Actarion turns existing SOPs and manuals into AI-generated, step-by-step digital work instructions. Operators follow them with AR guidance on a tablet or AR glasses, and every execution is logged for the audit trail.

### How does Actarion work with DeepSight?
Actarion is DeepSight's European partner. DeepSight provides the platform; Actarion handles local implementation, content guidance and change management in Benelux, DACH and the Nordics.

### What are digital work instructions?
Digital work instructions are step-by-step instructions on a screen instead of paper, with photos, video or 3D. See [digital work instructions](/en/solutions/digital-work-instructions/).

### How are AR SOPs different from paper instructions or an LMS?
AR SOPs appear in context at the machine, are easy to update and link execution data back to training and improvement. An LMS trains away from the work; AR guides during the work.

## Which use cases and devices fit?

### Which use cases can AI- and AR-guided work instructions help with?
Onboarding, changeovers, preventive maintenance, remote expert support, troubleshooting, quality inspections and safety procedures. See for example [changeover and SMED](/en/use-cases/changeover-smed/).

### Do we need smart glasses to start?
No. Many organizations start with smartphones or tablets. AR glasses such as Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2 or Android XR devices help mainly when the task needs both hands.

### How does AI help in practice?
AI creates draft instructions from your documents, photos or videos, generates multilingual versions and suggests steps and structure. Your experts keep final control.

### Does it support audits and compliance?
Yes. Each execution is logged with time, confirmations and deviations, and the skills matrix shows who is qualified. That supports audits under GMP, ISO 9001 or HACCP; validation follows your own quality system.

## How does implementation work?

### How quickly can we see value?
Most customers start with a focused pilot on one or two use cases. The no-code authoring tool lets you create and adjust content quickly.

### What does the Actarion approach look like?
Four phases: future-of-work briefing, use case discovery, pilot with fixed KPIs, and scale and enablement. See the [pilot approach](/en/pilot/).

### How do we keep control over quality and training?
Each person has a profile with completed instructions, training and certifications. Managers see where extra training is needed.

### Can our own people build and maintain instructions?
Yes. Enablement is part of the approach: we train your authors so you do not depend on us.
