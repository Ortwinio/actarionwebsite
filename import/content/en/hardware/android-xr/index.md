# Android XR in the enterprise: AR work instructions on Android devices

> Android XR is Google's operating system for extended reality headsets and glasses. The first device, Samsung Galaxy XR, launched in October 2025, and Google added Android Enterprise management for Android XR in 2026. For companies that already manage Android devices, Android XR is a new option for AR work instructions. Actarion assesses it per task.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for android xr enterprise over a control room](/images/hardware-android-xr-1600.webp)

## Why look at Android XR?

- Your fleet of scanners, phones and tablets already runs on Android.
- You need a HoloLens 2 successor and want more than one supplier to choose from.
- IT requires the same device management for headsets as for phones.
- You want both headsets and lighter glasses on one platform.

## What is Android XR?

Android XR is an Android-based platform for XR headsets and glasses, developed by Google with Samsung and Qualcomm. Apps from the Android ecosystem can run on it, and Google's Gemini AI is built in. Device makers can build both headsets and glasses on it.

## How do you test Android XR?

1. **Pick one task** where hands-free AR clearly helps.
2. **Check device management** with your IT and MDM vendor.
3. **Run the same instruction** on a tablet and an Android XR device.
4. **Measure** comfort, time and errors with operators.
5. **Decide** per task which device goes into scale-up.

## What does it bring each role?

| Role | Benefit |

## FAQ

### Is Android XR ready for industrial use?
The platform and enterprise management exist. Device choice is still limited, so test in a pilot first.

### Can Android XR replace HoloLens 2?
For many tasks it can be an option. See [HoloLens 2 alternatives](/en/hardware/hololens/).

### Do we need Android XR to use Actarion?
No. You can start on tablets and add headsets later.
