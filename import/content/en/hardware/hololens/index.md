# HoloLens 2 alternative: keep your AR work instructions running

> Microsoft has ended production of HoloLens 2 and will provide security updates until December 31, 2027. Teams that use HoloLens 2 need a HoloLens 2 alternative before then. Actarion helps you move AR work instructions to tablets, Apple Vision Pro or Android XR devices, and keeps using HoloLens 2 where it still runs.

![Pen drawing: work instructions move from an old AR headset to a tablet and a new headset](/img/illustraties/hardware-hololens.svg)

## What does the end of HoloLens 2 mean for you?

- No new units: broken devices are hard to replace.
- After 2027, no more security updates. Your IT will want the devices off the network.
- AR content built specifically for HoloLens may not run elsewhere.
- Operators have learned to work hands-free, and you do not want to go back to paper.

## What is the status of HoloLens 2?

In October 2024 Microsoft announced the end of HoloLens 2 production. Microsoft said the device would keep receiving updates for critical security issues and major regressions until December 31, 2027. Microsoft has not announced a successor for the enterprise market.

## How do you migrate from HoloLens 2?

1. **Inventory.** List the HoloLens apps, instructions and tasks in use.
2. **Separate content from device.** Move instructions to a device-independent platform.
3. **Choose per task.** Tablet for simple tasks, a new headset where hands-free is needed.
4. **Run in parallel.** Keep HoloLens 2 in use while you test the alternative.
5. **Phase out.** Retire devices before security updates stop.
