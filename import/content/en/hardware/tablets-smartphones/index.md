# Work instructions on tablet and smartphone: the practical start

> Work instructions on tablet are digital, step-by-step procedures shown on a tablet at the workstation or carried to the task. Most Actarion customers start on tablets: they are familiar, affordable and support AR through the camera. Smartphones cover field service and quick checks. AR glasses can be added later for hands-free tasks.

![A technician holds an iPad up to an electrical cabinet; the screen shows AR work instruction steps for work instructions on tablet](/images/hardware-tablets-smartphones-1600.webp)

## Why start on a tablet?

- Operators already know how to use a tablet. Training takes minutes.
- Many sites already have tablets and device management in place.
- You prove the value of digital instructions before investing in glasses.
- A tablet on a mount works at fixed workstations without anything to wear.

## What can a tablet do for work instructions?

A tablet shows each step with text, images, video and 3D models. Pointing the camera at the equipment shows AR markers on the screen. The operator confirms each step by tapping, adds photos as evidence and starts a [remote support](/en/platform/remote-support/) call when needed.

## Tablet, smartphone or AR glasses?

| Criterion | Tablet | Smartphone | AR glasses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen size | Large | Small | In field of view |
| Hands-free | With mount | No | Yes |
| Best for | Workstations, assembly, onboarding | Field service, quick checks | Maintenance, complex tasks |
| Threshold | Low | Very low | Higher |

See [AR glasses vs tablets](/en/compare/ar-glasses-vs-tablets/) for the full comparison.

## How do you set up work instructions on tablet?

1. **Choose the device.** Consumer tablet with a case, or a rugged industrial tablet for harsh areas.
2. **Choose the mount.** Arm, stand or wall bracket at the workstation; a strap or holster for mobile work.
3. **Check Wi-Fi.** Test coverage at every workstation.
4. **Install and manage.** Roll out the app through your mobile device management.
5. **Go live.** Start with one line and a handful of instructions.

## What does it bring each role?

| Role | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Operator | The right step on a familiar screen, at the workstation |
| Team leader | Fast rollout without long training |
| Quality / HSE | Confirmation and photos per step |
| HR / L&D | Onboarding at the real workstation |

## FAQ

### Can I use our existing tablets?
Usually yes, if they meet the minimum requirements. [TE BEVESTIGEN]

### Does AR work on a tablet?
Yes. The camera shows the equipment, with AR markers on top.

### Is a smartphone enough?
For short tasks and field service, often yes. For longer instructions, a tablet reads better.
