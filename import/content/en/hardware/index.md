# Smart glasses for manufacturing: choose the right device per task

> Smart glasses for manufacturing are head-worn displays that show work instructions in the operator's field of view, so both hands stay free. They are not always the best choice. Actarion runs the same AR work instructions on tablets, smartphones and AR headsets such as Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens 2 and Android XR devices, and helps you pick the device per task.

![Pen drawing: top view of a tablet, smartphone, AR glasses and headset, each showing a cyan work instruction](/img/illustraties/hardware.svg)

## What goes wrong when you start with the device?

- A pilot with expensive glasses fails because the task did not need hands-free work.
- Operators find a headset uncomfortable for a full shift.
- The chosen device is discontinued, and your content is tied to it.
- IT has no plan for charging, cleaning and managing shared devices.

## Which device fits which task?

| Device | Hands-free | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tablet](/en/hardware/tablets-smartphones/) | No | Workstations, assembly, inspections, onboarding | Needs a mount or a free hand |
| [Smartphone](/en/hardware/tablets-smartphones/) | No | Quick checks, field service, remote support | Small screen |
| [Apple Vision Pro](/en/hardware/apple-vision-pro/) | Yes | Training, complex assembly, high-detail 3D | Enclosed headset with passthrough video; weight |
| [HoloLens 2](/en/hardware/hololens/) | Yes | Existing installed base, maintenance | Production ended; security updates until end of 2027 |
| [Android XR devices](/en/hardware/android-xr/) | Yes | New deployments on Android-managed fleets | Young ecosystem; check device availability |

The devices supported by the platform are confirmed per project. [TE BEVESTIGEN: current device support list]

## How do you choose smart glasses for manufacturing?

1. **Start with the task.** Do operators need both hands? Do they move around? How long is the task?
2. **Check the environment.** Noise, dust, light, safety glasses, helmets and cleanroom rules.
3. **Start on a tablet.** It proves the instruction works before you add glasses.
4. **Test glasses on the hardest tasks.** Measure comfort, errors and time against the tablet.
5. **Plan fleet management.** Charging, hygiene, mobile device management and replacements.

## FAQ

### Are smart glasses worth it in manufacturing?
For hands-busy, complex or rare tasks, often yes. For simple workstation tasks, a tablet is usually enough.

### Can operators wear AR glasses with safety glasses?
It depends on the device and your PPE rules. Test this in the pilot.
