# Apple Vision Pro in the enterprise: AR work instructions and training

> Apple Vision Pro is a mixed reality headset that shows digital content in the user's surroundings through high-resolution passthrough video. For the enterprise, it fits training, complex assembly and tasks where detailed 3D content helps. Actarion uses Apple Vision Pro as one of the devices for AR work instructions, next to tablets and other headsets.

![A worker wearing Apple Vision Pro sees floating SOP panels for apple vision pro enterprise over a warehouse](/images/hardware-apple-vision-pro-1600.webp)

## Why are companies looking at Vision Pro?

- HoloLens 2 is no longer produced, and teams need a path forward.
- 3D models and video on a tablet are too small for complex assemblies.
- Training on real equipment is expensive or risky.
- Many companies already manage iPhones and iPads, so the Apple ecosystem is familiar to IT.

## What is Apple Vision Pro?

Apple Vision Pro runs visionOS. Unlike see-through glasses, it is a closed headset: cameras film the surroundings and show them on internal displays, with digital content mixed in. Control works with eyes, hands and voice. Apple released a version with the M5 chip in October 2025.

## Where does Vision Pro fit for work instructions?

| Fits well | Fits less well |
|---|---|
| Training and onboarding in a training cell | Full shifts on the line |
| Complex assembly with 3D models | Work in heavy dust, wet or hot areas |
| Design and engineering reviews | Tasks with mandatory safety glasses that do not fit under the headset |
| Rare maintenance tasks at a fixed spot | Moving across a large site |

## How do you run a Vision Pro pilot?

1. **Choose one task.** Pick a task where 3D guidance or hands-free work clearly helps.
2. **Check safety.** Assess field of view, cable and battery pack, and PPE with HSE.
3. **Build the instruction.** The same content can also run on a tablet for comparison.
4. **Test with operators.** Measure comfort, time and errors.
5. **Decide on scale.** Use the device where it wins; use tablets elsewhere.

## FAQ

### Is Apple Vision Pro suitable for manufacturing?
For training, complex assembly and engineering, yes. For long shifts in harsh areas, tablets or other devices are usually better.

### Can operators see the real machine?
Yes, through passthrough video. It is not optical see-through like HoloLens 2.

### What does Vision Pro cost for business use?
Device prices change; check with your Apple reseller. For the platform, book a free intake call.
