# How to measure the ROI of AR and AI guided work instructions

Calculating the return on investment (ROI) of AR and AI–guided work instructions is a clear requirement for operations, HSE, quality, and training managers. Industrial decision makers are under pressure to justify technology investments with hard numbers—especially when safety, uptime, and compliance are non-negotiable. Here’s how to approach ROI measurement for AR SOPs and digital work instructions, using practical metrics and proven methods.

## Why ROI matters for AR and AI–guided work instructions

Industrial leaders face a familiar challenge: traditional work instructions—paper manuals, static PDFs, even video libraries—are often outdated, hard to access, and prone to misinterpretation. This leads to:

- Variability in task execution
- Higher incident and error rates
- Slower onboarding and upskilling
- Inefficient troubleshooting and maintenance

When introducing AI and AR–guided work instructions, the question quickly becomes: do they deliver measurable value? For most organisations, this means:

- Reducing downtime and rework
- Improving compliance and audit readiness
- Accelerating technician onboarding and training
- Reducing safety incidents and near-misses
- Freeing up expert time for higher-value tasks

Proving ROI is not just a finance exercise—it’s how you secure budget, accelerate adoption, and drive culture change.

## The business case: why measure now?

Several trends make ROI measurement urgent for industrial companies:

- **Workforce turnover is rising.** According to [Deloitte’s 2023 Manufacturing Industry Outlook](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/energy-and-resources/articles/manufacturing-industry-outlook.html), skills gaps and retirements are straining technical teams.
- **Regulatory pressure is increasing.** Auditable, up-to-date work instructions are a core compliance requirement in sectors like energy, pharma, and aerospace.
- **Unplanned downtime is expensive.** For manufacturing, the average cost of a single hour of downtime can exceed $250,000 (Aberdeen Group, 2022).
- **Digital transformation budgets are under scrutiny.** Boards and finance teams demand clear metrics before greenlighting new technology.

Without a structured ROI approach, promising AR and AI initiatives risk stalling at the pilot stage.

## Core metrics for AR and AI–guided work instructions ROI

ROI for digital work instructions should be measured using operational, quality, and safety metrics that matter to your business. The main keyword—how to measure the ROI of AR and AI guided work instructions—means focusing on these areas:

### Productivity and uptime

- **Task execution time:** Measure the average time to complete standard procedures before and after AR SOP deployment.
- **Downtime reduction:** Track the frequency and duration of unplanned equipment outages.
- **First-time fix rate:** Quantify how often technicians resolve issues on the first attempt.

### Quality and compliance

- **Error and rework rates:** Log the number of process deviations, mistakes, or rework incidents.
- **Audit findings:** Count audit non-conformities related to procedures and documentation.
- **Standardisation:** Assess variation in how teams execute critical tasks.

### Safety and workforce readiness

- **Incident and near-miss rates:** Compare safety data before and after digital work instruction rollout.
- **Training time:** Measure the average time to onboard new technicians or upskill existing staff.
- **Certification pass rates:** Track outcomes for compliance-critical training modules.

### Cost and resource allocation

- **Manual handling costs:** Calculate paper/manual distribution, printing, and update labour.
- **Expert time saved:** Quantify reductions in senior engineer/technician callouts for routine support.
- **Travel and site visit reductions:** In multi-site operations, measure fewer expert dispatches due to remote AR support.

## How to calculate ROI: a step-by-step framework

To measure the ROI of AR and AI–guided work instructions, use a practical, stepwise approach:

### 1. Baseline your current performance

Start by collecting data on current performance for your target processes. Use:

- Maintenance logs and CMMS data
- Training and onboarding records
- Incident and audit reports
- Time and motion studies

This baseline is your reference point for improvement.

### 2. Define ROI objectives and KPIs

Set clear, quantifiable objectives for your AR and AI–guided work instructions project. Examples:

- Reduce average task execution time by 20%
- Cut onboarding time for new hires from 8 weeks to 5 weeks
- Lower rework incidents by 30% in critical operations

Link each objective to a measurable KPI. Engage stakeholders—maintenance leads, HSE, quality, L&D—to ensure buy-in.

### 3. Quantify benefits in financial terms

Translate operational improvements into financial savings:

- **Productivity gains:** Calculate hours saved per task × number of tasks × loaded labour rate.
- **Downtime reduction:** Estimate cost per hour of downtime avoided × hours saved.
- **Error reduction:** Multiply reduced rework cases by average cost per incident.
- **Training acceleration:** Factor in onboarding cost savings and faster time to productivity.

Example: If AR SOPs reduce average troubleshooting time by 30 minutes per incident across 500 incidents per year, at €60/hour loaded cost, that’s €15,000 annual savings.

### 4. Account for costs

Include all relevant costs:

- Hardware (AR headsets, tablets, etc.)
- Software licensing and support
- Content creation (converting SOPs to AR/AI format)
- Training and change management
- Ongoing maintenance and updates

Be transparent—this builds trust with finance and IT stakeholders.

### 5. Calculate payback and ROI

Use standard ROI formulas:

- **Simple payback period:** Total project cost / annual net benefit
- **ROI percentage:** (Net benefit – cost) / cost × 100

A payback period of 12–24 months is typical for industrial digital work instructions, provided the project is well-scoped.

### 6. Monitor, report, and refine

Establish ongoing tracking:

- Dashboards linking AR/AI platform data to KPIs
- Regular reviews with stakeholders
- Continuous improvement cycles (update SOPs, expand use cases)

Over time, this enables scaling from pilot to wider rollout.

## Use cases: ROI in practice

Several industrial sectors have successfully measured the ROI of AR and AI–guided work instructions. Here are concrete examples:

### Equipment maintenance in manufacturing

A global automotive supplier digitised maintenance SOPs using AR instructions. Results after six months:

- 25% reduction in average repair times
- 40% drop in unplanned downtime costs
- Rework incidents cut by 30%
- Payback in under 18 months

### Compliance-critical inspections in pharma

A pharmaceutical plant deployed AI and AR–guided inspections for cleanroom procedures:

- Audit non-conformities reduced from 8 to 2 per quarter
- Training time for new operators cut by 35%
- Consistent documentation and photo evidence for regulators
- ROI tracked using audit cost avoidance and training savings

### Remote support for field service teams

A utility company used AR–enabled remote guidance for field engineers:

- 50% fewer expert site visits required
- First-time fix rate improved by 18%
- Over €100,000 saved in travel and downtime in the first year

These results are typical where digital work instructions are embedded in daily operations, not just as a training tool.
