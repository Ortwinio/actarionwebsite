# How AR SOPs help you pass audits with less stress

Audits are a fact of life in regulated industries. Whether you face ISO, FDA, GMP or customer audits, the goal is always the same: demonstrate that your procedures are documented, followed consistently and verifiable. Yet for many organisations, audit preparation is a scramble—chasing signatures, digging through paper records and hoping nothing slips through the cracks.

AR SOPs and digital work instructions change this dynamic. By digitising procedures and capturing execution evidence automatically, they transform audits from stressful events into routine checkpoints.

## Why traditional SOPs make audits difficult

Most organisations still rely on paper-based SOPs, PDF documents and manual checklists. These methods have serious limitations when it comes to audit readiness:

- **Version control headaches:** Paper SOPs are hard to keep current. When auditors ask for proof that the latest version was used, teams scramble to find signed revision logs.
- **Incomplete evidence:** Manual checklists capture completion, but rarely capture how the work was done or whether it was done correctly.
- **Missing traceability:** Linking a specific execution to a specific operator, date, time and SOP version is often impossible with paper systems.
- **Last-minute panic:** Audit preparation becomes a crisis of gathering documents, tracking down signatures and hoping records exist.

For quality managers and compliance leaders, these gaps represent real risk. A single missing record or outdated SOP can trigger audit findings, corrective actions or even regulatory citations.

## How AR SOPs create audit-ready documentation

AR SOPs and digital work instructions address these challenges at the root. Here is how they support audit readiness:

### Automatic version control and distribution

When you publish an AR SOP, every user sees the latest, validated version—instantly. There is no confusion about which version is current, and no risk of operators using outdated procedures. Version history is logged and accessible for auditors.

### Evidence capture at every step

AR SOPs prompt operators to confirm each step and capture photos, videos or sensor readings as evidence. This creates a rich, timestamped record of exactly how the procedure was performed—not just that it was completed.

### Full traceability

Every AR SOP execution is linked to the operator, device, date, time and SOP version. Auditors can trace any completed procedure back to its source with a few clicks.

### Instant retrieval for audits

When auditors request records, you do not need to dig through filing cabinets or chase down paper logs. Digital records are searchable, exportable and audit-ready at all times.

## Real-world example: passing a GMP audit with AR SOPs

A pharmaceutical manufacturer faced a GMP audit with concerns about SOP consistency. Previous audits had flagged instances where operators followed outdated procedures or could not demonstrate step-by-step compliance.

By implementing AR SOPs with built-in verification and evidence capture, the company achieved:

- 100% traceability of SOP execution to operator and version
- Automatic photo capture at critical checkpoints
- Instant access to records during the audit itself

The audit proceeded smoothly, with no findings related to SOP compliance. Audit preparation time dropped by over 60%, freeing quality staff to focus on improvement rather than paperwork.

## Who benefits from AR SOPs for audit readiness?

AR SOPs for compliance and audits are relevant for:

- **Quality managers** who must demonstrate standardised, compliant execution
- **Operations managers** responsible for ensuring procedures are followed consistently
- **HSE managers** documenting safety-critical procedures and inspections
- **Compliance officers** preparing for regulatory, customer and internal audits
- **Auditors and inspectors** who appreciate clear, traceable documentation

## Getting started with AR SOPs for audit readiness

If you are considering AR SOPs to support compliance and audits, here are practical steps:

1. **Identify audit-critical procedures:** Focus first on SOPs that are frequently reviewed during audits or have a history of findings.
2. **Digitise and publish:** Convert paper SOPs into AR-guided procedures with checkpoints and evidence capture.
3. **Train teams:** Ensure operators understand how to execute AR SOPs and why evidence capture matters.
4. **Review and improve:** Use data from AR SOP executions to identify gaps, update procedures and strengthen compliance.
