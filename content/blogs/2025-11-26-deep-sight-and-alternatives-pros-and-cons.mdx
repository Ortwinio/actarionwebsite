# Deep Sight and alternatives: pros and cons for industrial AI and AR work instructions

Industrial operations are under pressure to deliver safer, more productive, and compliant performance. Many leaders are exploring AI and AR–guided work instructions to digitize standard operating procedures (SOPs), reduce errors, and accelerate workforce training. Among the options, Deep Sight and its alternatives offer different approaches to digital work instructions. Choosing the right solution can impact safety, quality, and operational efficiency for years to come.

## Why many industrial teams are evaluating Deep Sight and alternatives

Industrial teams face a complex mix of challenges:

- New equipment and technology demand rapid upskilling and error-free operation.
- Experienced technicians are retiring, and onboarding new hires must be fast and effective.
- Regulators require strict documentation and proof of compliance.
- Unplanned downtime, rework, and accidents drive up costs and risk.

In this context, digital work instructions—especially those enhanced by AI and augmented reality—address real business needs. Deep Sight, along with other platforms, promises to turn paper SOPs into step-by-step, interactive guidance delivered on smart devices or AR headsets.

But not all solutions are created equal. Decision makers want to know: What are the real pros and cons of Deep Sight and its competitors? Which platform fits best for their operational, safety, and training needs?

## The shift to AI and AR–guided work instructions

Traditional paper-based SOPs and static PDFs are no longer enough. They are hard to update, difficult to standardize, and often ignored in the field. This has led to:

- Inconsistent execution of critical tasks
- Difficulty capturing and sharing expert knowledge
- Gaps in compliance and audit trails

AI and AR–guided work instructions address these pain points by:

- Delivering step-by-step guidance, hands-free if needed
- Using visual overlays, checklists, and context-aware prompts
- Capturing data and proof of completion in real time

These digital approaches have shown measurable results:
- Up to 30% reduction in error rates ([Deloitte, 2021](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/focus/industry-4-0/using-augmented-reality-to-improve-manufacturing.html))
- 25–50% faster onboarding for new technicians
- Improved audit readiness and traceability

## Deep Sight: what it offers for industrial work instructions

Deep Sight positions itself as a platform for creating and delivering AR SOPs and digital work instructions. It targets industrial sectors such as manufacturing, energy, and field service. Key features typically include:

- Authoring tools for interactive SOPs, often with drag-and-drop interfaces
- AR overlays that guide technicians through complex tasks
- Integration with smart glasses and tablets
- Real-time data capture for compliance and analytics

**Pros of Deep Sight:**

- Accessible authoring tools for non-technical users
- Strong AR visualization and step guidance
- Support for hands-free wearable devices
- Analytics and reporting features for compliance

**Cons of Deep Sight:**

- Limited customization for highly specific workflows
- Hardware compatibility may be restricted (e.g., only certain smart glasses)
- Potential challenges integrating with legacy systems
- Licensing and scaling costs can increase rapidly for large teams

These factors make Deep Sight a reasonable starting point for teams seeking to digitize SOPs, but not always the best fit for every industrial environment.

## Key considerations when evaluating Deep Sight and alternatives

Every industrial site has unique requirements. Before selecting a platform, decision makers should consider:

- **Content creation:** How easy is it to convert existing SOPs and checklists into digital, AR–enabled formats? Can subject matter experts update content themselves?
- **Hardware support:** Does the solution work with your preferred devices (tablets, smart glasses, smartphones)? Can it run offline if needed?
- **Integration:** Will it connect to your maintenance systems (e.g., EAM, CMMS), learning management systems, or ERP?
- **Governance:** Does it provide version control, approval workflows, and audit trails for compliance?
- **Scalability:** Can it support multiple sites, languages, and user roles?
- **User adoption:** How intuitive is the interface for technicians and trainers? What support is available for onboarding and change management?
- **Cost and ROI:** Are licensing, implementation, and support costs transparent? What measurable benefits can you expect in productivity, quality, or safety?

## Addressing common objections: hardware, integration, and change management

Implementing AI and AR–guided work instructions involves practical hurdles:

- **Hardware:** Not all platforms support every device. Evaluate compatibility with your preferred tablets, smartphones, or smart glasses. Consider battery life, durability, and ease of use in your environment.
- **Integration:** Seamless data flow with maintenance, quality, or HR systems is essential. Ask vendors for concrete integration examples and references.
- **Content creation:** Platforms must empower your subject matter experts to update SOPs without IT bottlenecks. Look for intuitive authoring tools.
- **Change management:** Adoption depends on clear communication, hands-on training, and ongoing support. Choose a vendor with proven onboarding resources and industrial experience.
