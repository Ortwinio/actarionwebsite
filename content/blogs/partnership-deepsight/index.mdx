This partnership reinforces Deep Sight ’s ambition to accelerate growth in global markets and to extend its impact on industries such as manufacturing, logistics, and energy. 
With ActARion’s established network and expertise in the European market, Deep Sight is well-positioned to:
- **Expand its commercial footprint across Europe, bringing AR training to more industrial clients**
- **Strengthen local support to accelerate adoption and results**
- **Build long-term collaborations with European manufacturers and technology leaders**

“Europe is a critical growth market for Deep Sight , and partnering with ActARion enables us to bring our vision of smarter, safer, and more efficient training directly to European industries,” said Nicolas Bearzatto, President of Deep Sight . “This partnership marks a key step in our global growth strategy and highlights our commitment to building a strong, local presence in Europe and beyond.”

“ActARion’s partnership with Deep Sight marks an important milestone in our shared journey to make continuous improvements with augmented reality and AI possible,” said Ortwin Verreck, Managing Partner of ActARion. “By joining forces with Deep Sight , we can accelerate the adoption of augmented reality solutions across Europe, helping industrial operators reduce errors, improve safety, and transfer knowledge more effectively.”

## About Deep Sight – Augmented Reality Inc.
Deep Sight delivers a solution that transforms operations, training, and technical documentation in the manufacturing industry. Its platform enables organizations to capture and transfer critical know-how
effectively, leveraging AI and augmented reality. www.deepsight.ca

## About ActARion B.V.
ActARion, is specialized in Process improvement with AI and Augmented Reality and the exclusive partner of Deep Sight in DACH and Benelux. Based in the Netherlands and Switzerland, the company helps European industrial leaders adopt and scale AI and augmented reality solutions for training, onboarding, troubleshooting, and operational excellence. 

www.ActARion.com
